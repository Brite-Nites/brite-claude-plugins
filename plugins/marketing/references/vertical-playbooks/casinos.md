# Casinos vertical playbook

Brite-originated reference for all future casinos-vertical marketing work — outbound email copywriting, account research, situation mining, creative-angle generation, TAM mapping. Distills the BC-5930 research grounding into a reusable playbook so future skill authors don't re-run the research. Consumed by `email-copywriting` preset composition (BC-5934), `tam-mapping` when ported (BC-5946 / BC-5950), and `situation-mining` when ported.

Vertical scope is **US regional commercial + tribal Class III + racinos + riverboat / cruise-ship casinos** — four sub-types held on one playbook because the outbound motion shares Brite's "non-gaming retention infrastructure" worldview even when the buyer title, procurement protocol, and economics vary. Enterprise Strip flagships (Wynn, MGM Grand, Venetian, Bellagio, Caesars Palace) are out of scope per BC-5930 issue directive — centralized procurement, brand-standards compliance, multi-property RFPs, 12–18-month cycles make them a fundamentally different motion than mid-market independent outbound. Enterprise flagships surface in this playbook as ceiling-scale references (Moment Factory's [Resorts World Las Vegas GLOW](https://momentfactory.com/products/resorts-world-las-vegas), MK Illumination's [Shops at Crystals golden dragon at ARIA](https://www.mk-illumination.com/en-us)) but are not ICP.

Casino lighting is structurally different from zoos, hotels, and ski (see [`zoos.md`](./zoos.md), [`hotels-resorts.md`](./hotels-resorts.md), [`ski-resorts.md`](./ski-resorts.md) for peer playbook shapes). Zoos sell ticketed attendance against a sponsor-and-economic-dev stack; hotels sell rate-premium and booking-window extension against a revenue-management scorecard; ski resorts sell village-dwell extension and après-ski F&B per-cap lift against an ancillary-revenue scorecard. **Casinos sell non-gaming retention infrastructure — attach rate, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue share, comp-budget efficiency, year-round player-loyalty-tier programming — against the non-gaming P&L, outside the NIGC / state-commission gaming-regulated perimeter.** The offer library, persona surface, voice rules, and anti-slop list reflect those four structural differences. The 4-way ICP split is held on one playbook because Offer E (Production Finance) and Offer B (Retention Infrastructure Subscription) generalize across sub-types; the ICP-specific adjustments live in § Buyer personas + § Program economics + § Offer candidates.

Cross-reference for hotel-casino hybrids: if the prospect property leads with hotel branding and the casino is attached (e.g., resort-casino where hotel RevPAR is the primary P&L), [`hotels-resorts.md`](./hotels-resorts.md) drives the motion. If the prospect leads with casino branding and the hotel is attached (e.g., tribal gaming enterprise where gaming revenue is the primary P&L), this playbook drives.

## Vendor landscape (casinos lens)

The experiential-lighting vendor archetypes defined in [`../experiential-lighting-vendor-landscape.md`](../experiential-lighting-vendor-landscape.md) apply to casinos with three casino-specific adjustments: (a) a **mid-market casino AV installer cluster** surfaces as the dominant non-gaming AV procurement channel below Strip flagships — CCS Presentation Systems, Nanolumens, HB Communications; (b) the projection/immersive archetype (Moment Factory) is **adjacent not competitive at the in-scope tier**, with US installs concentrated exclusively at Strip and Boardwalk flagships; (c) lantern-festival producers are **decisively absent** at US casinos (venue topology + buyer mismatch). See the per-vertical framing audit subsection for MK Illumination and Moment Factory below.

**Christie Digital — dominant large-format projection and direct-view LED supplier across US casinos.** Christie self-documents casino penetration across control rooms, sportsbooks, lobby video walls, and exterior projection-mapping per [Christie's casino markets page](https://www.christiedigital.com/markets/casinos/). Named in-scope-adjacent installs include the [Westgate SuperBook 4,166 sq-ft LED wall built with 1,680 Velvet LED tiles](https://www.commercialintegrator.com/projects/100-feed-video-wall-westgate-christie/48780/) (Strip adjacency) and [Casino Arizona / Talking Stick Resort rotunda plus 100-display property-wide deployment](https://ccsprojects.com/portfolio/talking-stick-resort/) (tribal in-scope). Positioning: Christie sells the **display-and-projection layer**, not the programmable-media-narrative layer Brite occupies. Adjacent-and-complementary at the enterprise scale; direct-overlap risk only if Christie partners with a creative studio for an exterior projection-mapping engagement that lands in Brite's pool-deck / porte-cochere zone.

**Mid-market casino AV installer cluster (vertical-unique archetype, BC-5921 task-3 extension).** This archetype does not exist in the shared `experiential-lighting-vendor-landscape.md` because it is casino-specific — the confluence of surveillance-AV, sportsbook-LED, and non-gaming-amenity AV at a single property creates an installer tier that bundles all three under one master-service agreement. **CCS Presentation Systems** executed the [Casino Arizona / Talking Stick Resort deployment with 1,000+ speakers, 100 displays, largest SVSi N3000 system in the US (46 inputs / 101 outputs)](https://ccsprojects.com/portfolio/talking-stick-resort/) and runs the [23-property Choctaw Nation digital-signage portfolio](https://ccsprojects.com/growing-audio-visual-solutions-in-casinos/). **Nanolumens** partnered on the Talking Stick Lobby Rotunda direct-view LED (per [Digital Signage Today](https://www.digitalsignagetoday.com/news/casino-arizonas-talking-stick-property-taps-nanolumens-digital-signage-tech/)). **HB Communications** executed the Westgate Super Book alongside Christie. These three are the named named mid-market incumbents; Diversified and AVI-SPL operate at the ~$1B-revenue enterprise tier ([Commercial Integrator](https://www.commercialintegrator.com/business_resources/operations/billion-dollar-av-diversified-avi-spl/)) but surface at Strip flagships, not regional / tribal / racino / riverboat in primary research at this depth. Positioning for Brite: the mid-market installer cluster owns the **infrastructure / digital-signage-hardware layer** — Brite's programmable-media-narrative-plus-loyalty-tier-integration layer sits on top of or adjacent to it. When account research surfaces a CCS / Nanolumens / HB incumbent, position Brite as the programmable-media partner that works WITH the installer, not a displacement bid.

**Moment Factory — adjacent not competitive at the in-scope tier (per-vertical audit; see below).** Named US casino installs are **exclusively Strip and Boardwalk flagships**: [Resorts World Las Vegas "GLOW" (2022, property-wide, 100,000+ sq-ft LED across West Tower / East Tower / Zouk / 50-ft interior globe)](https://momentfactory.com/products/resorts-world-las-vegas) and [Tropicana Atlantic City boardwalk facade (2015, 100m facade, column LEDs + ribbon + light cannons)](https://momentfactory.com/products/tropicana-atlantic-city). Moment Factory's own [casinos collection](https://momentfactory.com/collections/casinos) adds Casino de Montréal (Canada) and Pasino Grand (France). **No tribal, regional, racino, or riverboat US deployments surface.** Engagement floor is well above regional / tribal / racino / riverboat capex norms.

**MK Illumination — adjacent not competitive at the in-scope tier; active-watch flag (per-vertical audit; see below).** Single US casino-adjacent deployment is the [Shops at Crystals golden dragon at Simon Property Group's Las Vegas Strip retail center — >100,000 points of light, produced by MK Illumination USA for Western Design LLC](https://www.mk-illumination.com/en-us) — a *Strip retail-corridor install inside ARIA*, not a regional / tribal / racino / riverboat engagement. MK's broader US portfolio concentrates at shopping centers and retail parks ([Saks Fifth Avenue NY Frozen 2 lightshow 2019](https://www.mk-illumination.com/en-us)).

**Lantern-festival producers — absent / rare at US casinos.** [Tianyu Arts & Culture positions around zoos, botanical gardens, and fairgrounds](https://tianyuculture.us/about-us/). No US tribal commercial or regional casino Tianyu deployment surfaces. Enchant and Illuminight remain unverified at casinos. Structural reason: lantern-festival pathway format needs flat parkable 6–8-week footprints; casino properties favor permanent programmable-media infrastructure wired into year-round loyalty programming, not limited-run pathway attractions.

**Theming specialists (Thinkwell, BRC Imagination Arts, Sparks) — adjacent, not casino-specialists.** Public portfolios concentrate on theme parks, museums, and corporate brand attractions per [BRC positioning](https://www.brcweb.com/about/); no named US casino installs surface in research at this depth. Treat as adjacent-not-competitive; if a casino signs a theming specialist for a standalone attraction inside the property, positioning shifts to "programmable-media backbone WITH the theming attraction," not competitive.

**LED-retrofit vendors — adjacent, same framing as zoos / hotels / ski.** Goodlight / Orphek / EcoMedia serve Facilities / engineering buyers on capex energy-reduction cycles. Wrong buyer (Facilities, not Non-Gaming Operations), wrong P&L (capex energy reduction, not non-gaming retention programming), wrong motion. Surfaces in "lighting for casinos" searches and must be set aside.

### Explicitly adjacent, not competitive (casinos-specific) — mandatory per-vertical framing audit

Two vendor names require a casinos-specific ruling that confirms the pattern and records the specific outcome for this vertical. This is the **3rd instance of the cross-vertical framing audit established by BC-5921 task-1 (hotels MK Illumination inversion) and extended by BC-5922 task-1 (ski Moment Factory inversion)** — elevating the audit pattern from pattern-application to architecture. See `docs/precedents/BC-5921.md` + `docs/precedents/BC-5922.md` for the inversion mechanism. **Casinos ruling: both MK and Moment Factory are adjacent-not-competitive at the in-scope tier**, which matches zoos and does not match hotels (MK inverted) or ski (Moment Factory inverted). The audit outcome is "no inversion at this vertical" — the pattern generalizes that each vertical gets its own ruling, not that inversions always happen.

**MK Illumination — adjacent at US in-scope casinos; active-watch on attached shopping-corridor build-outs.** Unlike the BC-5921 hotels inversion (where MK IS a direct hospitality competitor at destination-resort tier), at US regional / tribal / racino / riverboat casinos **MK is adjacent not competitive**. The one named US casino-adjacent install (Shops at Crystals at ARIA) is a retail-corridor-inside-Strip-flagship pattern, not an in-scope-tier pattern. Classify MK as adjacent-not-competitive with an **active-watch flag**: if a tribal or regional operator builds an attached enterprise-retail-landlord-branded shopping arcade (Simon / Brookfield / Taubman-tier), MK becomes a direct rival in that narrow sleeve. Any casino-vertical preset composition that lifts the BC-5921 hotels "MK is a direct competitor" framing without regional / tribal / racino / riverboat MK evidence should be sent back.

**Moment Factory — adjacent at US in-scope casinos; ceiling-reference in outbound copy.** Unlike the BC-5922 ski inversion (where Moment Factory IS the primary competitor at ski resorts with three named Lumina installs), at US regional / tribal / racino / riverboat casinos **Moment Factory is adjacent not competitive**. The two named US casino installs (Resorts World Las Vegas GLOW, Tropicana Atlantic City facade) are both Strip/Boardwalk flagships; Moment Factory's engagement floor is well above in-scope-tier capex norms. Reframe Moment Factory in outbound copy as a **ceiling reference**: "the [Resorts World GLOW](https://momentfactory.com/products/resorts-world-las-vegas) pattern, productized for your scale and your carded-player program." Any casino-vertical preset composition that lifts the BC-5922 ski "Moment Factory is the primary competitor" framing without tribal / regional / racino / riverboat Moment Factory evidence should be sent back.

**S4 Lights — supply-chain partner, not competitor.** Same framing as at zoos / hotels / ski. Brite is a partner with S4 (per BC-5879 zoos-ledger § A3 case studies). When S4 surfaces in casino prospect research, the framing is supply-chain credibility, not competitive overlap.

## Buyer personas

The 4-way ICP scope forces **four parallel primary personas**, one per sub-type, because the decision surface is structurally different at regional commercial vs tribal Class III vs racinos vs riverboat / multi-property. The **Director of Food & Beverage** holds across all four sub-types as a shared secondary signer with native P&L authority over the experiential-programming zone. The persona set **departs from the BC-5930 issue body directive**, which prescribed "COO / VP Operations / VP Non-Gaming Revenue / Director of F&B" as primaries across all tiers — R-6 title-prevalence research found VP Non-Gaming Revenue effectively unverified as a formal title outside integrated-resort flagships, and the non-gaming P&L ownership varies meaningfully across the four sub-types. See § Flagged departure from issue body below.

**Primary 1 — General Manager (GM) + VP Casino Operations (regional commercial).** Owns property-level P&L, signs vendor contracts, and at mid-market regional properties (Monarch Black Hawk, Full House Chamonix, Full House American Place, Penn regional properties, Boyd Ameristar portfolio, Eldorado / Bally's regionals, Golden Entertainment, Century Casinos) routinely serves as the non-gaming decision authority. VP Casino Operations sits above the property GM at multi-property operators. Cares about carded-player attach rate, non-gaming revenue share, loyalty-tier retention, F&B per-cap, seasonal-compression-window amplification. Signs **Offer E (Production Finance)** as primary decision authority and authorizes **Offer A (Pilot Zone)** directly. Fears signing: unrecoverable non-gaming capex without a defensible carded-player-attach or comp-budget-efficiency story; regional-VP pushback on vendor spend that doesn't move the non-gaming scoreboard.

**Primary 2 — Casino GM + Executive Officer Gaming & Hospitality + Tribal Council capex-gate (tribal Class III).** Casino GM is the operator-level decision-maker at a single tribal property; multi-property tribal enterprises surface a tribal-enterprise-level hospitality officer — Choctaw names an "Executive Officer, Gaming & Hospitality" per [tgandh.com Heidi Grant profile](https://tgandh.com/articles/the-largest-expansion-in-the-southwest-qa-with-heidi-grant-executive-officer-gaming-hospitality-choctaw-nation-of-oklahoma/). **Tribal Council capex-gate is a vertical-unique archetype** (documented inline per BC-5921 task-3 precedent because it does not exist in the shared `experiential-lighting-vendor-landscape.md`): the Tribal Council approves property budgets annually per [NIGC regulatory framework](https://www.nigc.gov/commission/frequently-asked-questions/), and experiential-programming capex above a tribe-specific dollar threshold requires Council agenda placement. **The specific dollar threshold is tribe-specific and NOT publicly standardized** — discover-per-account during qualification, do not assume a canonical $250K / $500K / $1M number. Tribal enterprises named in research: Foxwoods (Mashantucket Pequot), Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood + Tampa (Seminole Tribe), Yaamava' (San Manuel), Choctaw Casinos & Resorts (Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma), Pechanga, Morongo, Soaring Eagle, Barona, Sycuan. Signs **Offer E** as primary with Tribal Council endorsement; authorizes **Offer A** at GM level if within Council-delegated authority. Outbound etiquette: address the tribe as a sovereign (not as a private commercial buyer); expect a Tribal Council / Tribal Gaming Commission procurement gate in addition to property-level approval; favor tribally-owned or 8(a) / Buy-Indian-Act-eligible subcontracting where applicable ([Harvard Undergraduate Law Review 2025](https://hulr.org/fall-2025/reclaiming-authority-the-case-for-tribal-economic-sovereignty-in-the-gaming-era)).

**Primary 3 — Director of Casino Operations (racinos).** Casino-side specifically — **racing-side operations are a different P&L and a different buyer** (Director of Racing / General Manager of Racing) and are explicitly out-of-scope for Brite's experiential-programming motion. At Yonkers Empire City the casino-side leadership signs for the $2.3B full-casino-conversion program ([Yonkers Times](https://yonkerstimes.com/mgm-empire-citys-2-3-billion-expansion-could-double-yonkers-revenue-and-jobs/)); at Harrah's Philadelphia the casino GM reports into the Caesars regional structure. Program scope at racinos tends to be smaller than at Tribal Class III or full-scale regional commercial — non-gaming zones are typically narrower (no resort pool deck, no multi-tower hotel facade), so Offer A pilot-zone economics often fit better than Offer E full-property orchestration at the racino tier.

**Primary 4 — Regional VP of Operations (riverboat / multi-property).** At Boyd Gaming, Penn Entertainment, Bally's, and Caesars regional portfolios, non-gaming P&L authority sits at the Regional VP layer — one executive overseeing multiple riverboat and small-commercial properties. Named operator context: Penn's five-segment regional structure (Northeast / South / West / Midwest / Interactive, [Q4 2025 release](https://investors.pennentertainment.com/news-releases/news-release-details/penn-entertainment-inc-reports-third-quarter-results-and)); Boyd's portfolio anchored by Ameristar St. Charles ([property site](https://ameristarstcharles.boydgaming.com/)). Regional VP signs corporate-portfolio-level programs (multi-property Offer E); property GM signs single-property pilots (Offer A). Outbound-sequencing implication: pitch Regional VP with a multi-property-rollout hypothesis OR pitch individual property GMs with single-property pilot fit — don't mix framings.

**Secondary (shared across all 4 sub-types) — Director of Food & Beverage.** F&B Director is the primary experiential-programming P&L owner at the zone level across every sub-type — pool deck, porte-cochere, F&B patio, retail promenade, hotel tower exterior. Confirmed reporting structure: [F&B Director reports to the GM and serves as Manager on Duty at Walker's Bluff / Kickapoo Lucky Eagle / Rivers Casino and peer properties per CCS-documented ops](https://ccsprojects.com/portfolio/talking-stick-resort/). The per-cap / attach-lift pitch lands here as the numerate evaluator who can quantify what a programmable non-gaming layer does to F&B per-cap, tenant retention, and carded-player cross-sell. Primary 1–4 will route the vendor deck to F&B Director for attach-math review before signing. Pitch F&B Director as co-primary at properties with dedicated F&B-directed non-gaming expansion (Foxwoods Grace by Nia supper club + Hell's Kitchen, Seminole Hard Rock 20+ F&B outlets, Pechanga's 13 restaurants, Chamonix 980 Prime steakhouse).

**Explicitly NOT a target — per BC-5930 issue directive.** Marketing (casino marketing sells comp offers and loyalty-tier promotions, not infrastructure) and Facilities (facilities buys capex energy retrofits, not non-gaming retention programming) are the two default-excluded persona surfaces. Outbound that lands at Marketing reads as "vendor doesn't know what they're selling"; outbound that lands at Facilities routes the email to the wrong P&L entirely. Edge case: tribal casinos sometimes consolidate multiple non-gaming functions under a "Facilities and Non-Gaming" director at small properties — confirm per-account during qualification, but default is excluded.

### Flagged departure from issue body (BC-5922 task-2 3rd-instance pre-compose gate)

The BC-5930 issue body names four primary personas — COO / VP Operations / VP Non-Gaming Revenue / Director of F&B — as a single persona list across all casino sub-types. R-6 title-prevalence research confirmed: (1) VP Non-Gaming Revenue is effectively unverified as a formal title outside integrated-resort flagships per [LinkedIn "VP Casino" job search returning 2,000+ jobs with "VP Non-Gaming Revenue" construction not surfacing as standard](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/vp-casino-jobs); (2) the primary non-gaming P&L operator title varies by sub-type (GM + VP Casino Operations at regional commercial; Casino GM + Executive Officer Gaming & Hospitality + Tribal Council at tribal Class III; Director of Casino Operations at racinos; Regional VP of Operations at riverboat / multi-property); (3) Director of F&B holds as shared secondary across all four sub-types. Playbook names VP Non-Gaming Revenue only where the title exists (Caesars / MGM / Wynn flagships + Hard Rock / Penn / Boyd enterprise-regional tier); **in-scope-tier primary personas are sub-type-dependent per above**. **Departure approved at brainstorm gate 2026-04-23 per BC-5922 task-2 precedent** (3rd instance of pre-compose persona gate, elevating pattern from pattern-application to architecture — see `docs/precedents/BC-5922.md` + this-issue precedent file at ship time).

## Recency signals casinos leak publicly

Casinos leak signals through gaming trade press ([GGB Magazine](https://ggbmagazine.com/), [CDC Gaming Reports](https://cdcgaming.com/), [Indian Gaming Magazine](https://www.casinocitypress.com/indiangaming/), [Tribal Gaming and Hospitality Magazine](https://tgandh.com/)), 10-K / 10-Q filings at publicly traded operators (Penn, Boyd, Caesars, Churchill Downs, Monarch, Full House), SEC 8-K event filings, tribal-enterprise press, state-gaming-commission meeting minutes, and regional business press. Seven recency signals matter; decay windows cross-reference [`../shelf-life-patterns.md`](../shelf-life-patterns.md).

**Non-gaming expansion + capital-plan announcements** (decay: 6–12 months, strongest in first 6). The single highest-signal recency surface. Named named recent expansions at in-scope properties: [Foxwoods $385M expansion anchored by $300M Great Wolf Lodge opening June 15, 2025 + Pequot Woodlands Casino + Hell's Kitchen + Grace by Nia supper club](https://www.casino.org/news/foxwoods-embarks-on-385m-expansion-as-anniversary-celebration-continues/); [Seminole Hard Rock Tampa $65M upgrade completed January 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/seminole-hard-rock-hotel--casino-hollywood-unveils-a-new-holiday-tradition-hard-rock-the-holidays-a-family-friendly-light-show-spectacular-at-the-guitar-hotel-302602648.html); [Yaamava' / San Manuel $760M expansion + 2025 cashless rollout](https://www.indiangaming.com/yaamava-resort-casino-at-san-manuel-holds-grand-opening-celebration/); [Choctaw Sky Tower $600M + Choctaw Landing Hochatown early-2024 open](https://tgandh.com/articles/the-largest-expansion-in-the-southwest-qa-with-heidi-grant-executive-officer-gaming-hospitality-choctaw-nation-of-oklahoma/); [Pechanga $285M expansion with 13 restaurants + 4.5-acre Cove pool complex](https://blogs.pechanga.com/newsroom/pechanga-tops-massive-285-million-resort-expansion/); [Monarch Black Hawk $400M 516-room tower + Spa Monarch 2024](https://monarch-casino.mandccommunications.com/monarch-casino-resort-spa-celebrates-the-grand-opening-of-its-400-million-expansion); [Full House Chamonix December 2023 open + 980 Prime steakhouse March 2024](https://investors.fullhouseresorts.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/295/chamonix-colorados-newest-and-most-luxurious-casino); [Yonkers Empire City $2.3B full-casino conversion targeting 2027 open with 112-ft BetMGM LED sportsbook screen](https://yonkerstimes.com/mgm-empire-citys-2-3-billion-expansion-could-double-yonkers-revenue-and-jobs/). Post-opening 6–18-month window opens the non-gaming-programming capital conversation.

**F&B concept launches + executive chef hires** (decay: 3–6 months). New restaurant, bar, lounge, or supper-club openings surfaced via tribal-enterprise press (Foxwoods Grace by Nia March 2023) + regional business press + trade publications. Tenant churn is a bad-health signal; new tenant signings are a non-gaming-revenue-investment signal. Executive chef hires signal F&B-programmatic-investment windows (strongest signal at multi-outlet properties like Seminole Hard Rock 20+ outlets, Pechanga 13 restaurants).

**New GM / COO / Executive Officer Gaming & Hospitality appointments** (decay: 6–12 months, strongest in first 6). GGB's executive-appointments column + [Indian Gaming Magazine's monthly coverage](https://www.casinocitypress.com/indiangaming/) + tribal-enterprise press track appointments. New GMs run non-gaming-audit cycles in months 3–9 of tenure; outbound landing in that window fits the audit cadence. LinkedIn role-change alerts + property PR are the primary surfaces.

**Tribal Council capital-plan approvals + annual budget agenda items** (decay: 12–24 months). Tribal Councils approve property budgets annually per [NIGC framework](https://www.nigc.gov/commission/frequently-asked-questions/); meeting minutes + tribal-press announcements + 8(a) / Buy-Indian-Act procurement notices surface the capital-planning window. Tribal-specific: the Council agenda cycle is the outbound-timing constraint, not the property-level quarterly cycle.

**State tourism-bureau + economic-development partnership announcements** (decay: 12+ months). State tourism bureaus + destination-marketing organizations + economic-development agencies sometimes partner with casinos on tourism-draw programming. [Native American Business Development Institute (NABDI) grants](https://www.bia.gov/service/grants/nabdi) underwrite feasibility studies; [US Economic Development Administration tribal-community resources](https://www.eda.gov/resources/resources-for-tribes-and-tribal-governments) create partnership adjacencies. Direct grant-funding-casino-experiential-capex precedent did not surface in R-6 research — unverified but plausible; discover-per-account.

**Loyalty-tier-gated programming press** (decay: evergreen pattern, refresh annually). [Hard Rock's "Hard Rock the Holidays" ties programming access to Unity by Hard Rock member pricing at $35 versus general admission](https://casino.hardrock.com/hollywood/events/hard-rock-the-holidays) — the single cleanest public precedent of year-round / seasonal architectural programmable-media programming tied to player-loyalty-tier promotions. When a property announces a loyalty-tier-integrated program, it signals the property has both the carded-player-infrastructure AND the architectural-programming appetite Brite's Offer B (Retention Infrastructure Subscription) slots into.

**10-K / 10-Q non-gaming segment disclosures** (decay: quarterly). Public-operator disclosures that break out non-gaming revenue or F&B / hotel / entertainment segments inform the attach-rate + visitor-per-trip economics. Named disclosures: [Caesars 2024 10-K reports F&B at ~15% of total net revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590895/000159089525000068/czr-20241231.htm); [Boyd Gaming 2024 10-K reports gaming at 66% with F&B + rooms + other each <8% individually](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/boyd-gaming-reports-fourth-quarter-full-year-2024-results-302370538.html). Penn Entertainment reports by geography (five regional segments) rather than gaming/non-gaming split. Churchill Downs consolidates Gaming + F&B with comp-adjustment methodology per [Q1 2026 10-Q](https://last10k.com/sec-filings/chdn/0000020212-25-000051.htm).

## Program economics

Anchor numbers for casino outbound work. Every number below is either public-source (cited) or flagged unverified. **Multiple casino economics anchor-numbers are unverified in primary public sources** — flag and discover-per-account during qualification rather than citing canonical benchmarks.

**Non-gaming revenue share (directional).** [Boyd 2024 10-K implies aggregate non-gaming (F&B + rooms + other, excluding online) in the 25–34% band at a regional-commercial operator](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/boyd-gaming-reports-fourth-quarter-full-year-2024-results-302370538.html); Caesars' F&B alone runs ~15% of total net revenue. [Marina Bay Sands' 65% non-gaming share](https://ggbmagazine.com/) is the integrated-resort ceiling (out-of-scope but useful as ceiling anchor). The widely-cited "30–50% non-gaming share at regional" benchmark is **directionally supported but not precisely sourced in AGA reports** — flag as likely-true-but-best-in-class-citation-needed. Use "at regional, non-gaming typically runs 25–40% of total revenue per public-operator filings" rather than a pinpoint number in outbound copy.

**F&B attach-rate benchmarks.** Property-specific F&B attach rates (percentage of gaming visitors who also purchase F&B) are **unverified in public sources** at primary-research depth. [UNLV Gaming Research & Review Journal published "Evaluating the Impact of a New Resort Amenity on Gaming Business Volumes"](https://oasis.library.unlv.edu/grrj/vol14/iss2/1/) — a pool / nightclub amenity significantly increased table-game drop at $150,500 per day; directional evidence that non-gaming amenities lift gaming volumes. Frame F&B-attach in outbound copy as "we'd benchmark against your carded-player attach curve" rather than citing a canonical benchmark.

**Comp-budget as % of non-gaming cost.** The 30–45% industry range cited in hospitality-industry commentary is **unverified in public sources at primary-research depth**. [NIGC confirms comp items are part of the annual Tribal Council-approved budget](https://www.nigc.gov/commission/frequently-asked-questions/); [Cherokee Nation Gaming Commission Tribal Internal Control Standards](https://www.cherokee.org/media/bskik4v0/tribal-internal-control-standards.pdf) formalize comp-approval structure. Reference the mechanism (comp-budget-offset as a metric) without a precise industry benchmark. Offer F (Comp-Multiplier) rests on a measurement-infrastructure hypothesis — Brite's comp-rate-reduction metric is a discovered property-specific number, not a canonical benchmark.

**Ticket / admission pricing anchors.** Loyalty-tier-gated non-gaming programs price below general-admission to drive carded-player acquisition: [Hard Rock Hollywood "Hard Rock the Holidays" 2025 pricing at $35 for Unity by Hard Rock members](https://casino.hardrock.com/hollywood/events/hard-rock-the-holidays) versus general-admission pricing. The loyalty-tier differential IS the offer — programmable-media programming becomes the acquisition-funnel entry point for the player-loyalty program, not a standalone revenue line.

**Program scale benchmarks (in-scope precedents).**

## Good-vs-bad program taxonomy

Five dimensions distinguish casino non-gaming programs that compound (carded-player attach lift, loyalty-tier retention, multi-year YoY refresh, comp-budget efficiency) from programs that burn out (operational-only F&B decor, gaming-floor-overrun compliance escalations, one-and-done holiday installs, capex-sunk buildouts with no programmable overlay). Dimensions derived from R-6 research grounding + Brite cross-vertical discipline. The 5-dimension frame is also the preset-composition guide (Brite's zone is the compounding quadrant; anti-slop rules enforce the boundary against the burnout quadrant).

**Dimension 1 — Zone-by-zone in / out boundary (the permission-to-sell gate).** This dimension is load-bearing because it answers the compliance question upstream of every other decision.

**IN (Brite's zone) — the widest non-gaming footprint at the property:**
- **F&B zones (primary anchor zone):** restaurants, bars, lounges, buffets, supper clubs, patio F&B. Owned by Director of F&B P&L; primary zone for Offer A pilot-zone motion. Anchor properties: Foxwoods Grace by Nia + Hell's Kitchen + Pequot Woodlands, Seminole Hard Rock 20+ F&B outlets, Pechanga 13 restaurants, Chamonix 980 Prime.
- **Hotel-attached zones (lobby, pool deck, spa, hotel tower exterior, hallways):** IN for casino-first properties (casino-branded enterprise with attached hotel). **Cross-reference rule** per opening paragraph: if prospect leads with hotel branding, [`hotels-resorts.md`](./hotels-resorts.md) drives; if prospect leads with casino branding, this playbook drives. Anchor properties: Seminole Hard Rock Guitar Hotel 24-acre pool, Pechanga Cove 4.5-acre pool complex, Monarch Black Hawk Spa Monarch, Chamonix 300-guestroom hotel tower.
- **In-house entertainment venues (theaters, concert halls, nightclubs, supper clubs with live programming):** IN but **Offer A pilot only** (single-venue scope). Brite programmable-media backbone WITH the AV integrator incumbent is the positioning, not displacement. Anchor properties: Yaamava' Theater (Live Nation partnership, 2,600–3,000 seats), Hard Rock Live Hollywood (2025 Pollstar Casino/Resort Venue of the Year), Boyd Ameristar RYSE EDM nightclub + Bottleneck Blues Bar.
- **Convention / conference space:** IN with AV-integrator-adjacency caveat — event-specific AV integrators (Encore, PSAV, Diversified) typically serve this zone well for event-by-event programming. Brite plays where the venue has programmable-media ambition beyond event-scoped AV — permanent ambient programmable-media that carries across events. Anchor example: Ameristar St. Charles 20,000-sq-ft conference center.
- **Retail promenades + arcades:** IN. MK Illumination is the adjacency concern at attached-shopping-corridor builds (see § Vendor landscape — if a tribal or regional operator builds an enterprise-retail-landlord-branded arcade, MK becomes a narrow-zone rival). For tribal-enterprise-operated retail, Brite's programmable-media-plus-loyalty-tier integration is the differentiator over decorative holiday lighting.
- **Arrival / parking structures / porte-cochere:** IN. High-impact brand-touchpoint zones where programmable-media architectural lighting sets the non-gaming-narrative frame before the guest enters the gaming floor. Native Brite fit; no in-scope-tier competitor incumbent.
- **Boardwalk facades / exterior plazas / hotel-tower exteriors:** IN. Seminole Hard Rock Guitar Hotel tower, Tropicana Atlantic City boardwalk facade (ceiling reference), Choctaw Sky Tower 21-story exterior. The architectural-canvas tier — Offer E Production Finance primary zone.

**OUT (explicitly NOT Brite's zone, compliance-regulated perimeter):**
- **Gaming floor, pit, table games, slot arrays, count room, cage, surveillance rooms.** Regulated by [NIGC Minimum Internal Control Standards (MICS) for tribal Class III](https://www.nigc.gov/news/detail/nigc-announce-the-approval-of-guidance-on-the-class-iii-minimum-internal-co) + state gaming commissions (Nevada, Mississippi, Louisiana, Missouri, Illinois, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, New York, Michigan, etc.) for commercial. Surveillance-adequacy requirements govern lighting indirectly; count-room access and cage protocols are regulated directly. **Zero Brite pitch in these zones — full stop.** Outbound that proposes gaming-floor programmable-media reads as vendor fundamentally mis-understanding the regulatory perimeter and collapses trust instantly.

**Dimension 2 — Carded-player attach-lift vs one-time-admission pricing.** Good programs integrate with the carded-player / loyalty program infrastructure — Hard Rock Hollywood's Unity-tier-gated pricing ($35 member vs. general admission) IS the mechanism. Programmable-media programs become the acquisition funnel for the player-loyalty program, not a standalone revenue line. Bad programs price flat for all visitors, extract one-time F&B attach, and leave the loyalty-conversion opportunity on the table. This dimension is the single strongest predictor of year-2 program compounding at casinos.

**Dimension 3 — Static vs programmable / dynamic.** Good: DMX-pixel programmable canvas that refreshes with daily / weekly / seasonal content (loyalty-tier promotion tie-ins, compression-week amplification, F&B-concept thematic cross-promotions). Bad: static LED strings, uplit facade, decorative holiday-installer-tier installs — age poorly, produce "been there" effect after year two, lose loyalty-program-integration opportunity. Boyne Mountain SkyBridge's 200K individually-controllable LED nodes (cited in [ski-resorts.md](./ski-resorts.md)) is the programmable-standard cross-vertical anchor; Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood 2M+ LEDs is the casino-in-scope precedent.

**Dimension 4 — Multi-season / year-round vs holiday-only.** Good: programmable infrastructure carries across Q4 holidays + compression-week events + F&B-concept launches + shoulder-season loyalty-tier promotions — the infrastructure amortizes year-round. Bad: holiday-decor installer with 6-week pivot and 10-month idle infrastructure. Hard Rock Hollywood's 35-day Nov-to-Jan run is at the holiday-concentrated end of the spectrum but the Guitar Hotel Light & Music Show infrastructure is a permanent platform that enables year-round expansion — good execution of a dimension that could easily be bad.

**Dimension 5 — Vendor tenure + site-switching cost.** Good: multi-year accretive vendor relationships with YoY additions — the pattern at Seminole Hard Rock's multi-year programming buildout and Choctaw's 23-property digital-signage portfolio compounding over time. Bad: holiday-decor installer-of-the-year rotations, no compounding narrative, no platform lock-in, every RFP starts from scratch. The mid-market casino AV installer cluster (CCS / Nanolumens / HB Communications) creates *infrastructure lock-in*, which Brite's programmable-media-narrative layer converts into *programming lock-in* — the compounding moat lives in the programming layer, not the infrastructure layer.

## Offer candidates

**Four offer candidates, one anchored to each of the four worldview drivers** (attach rate, retention infrastructure, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue, comp-rate reduction). Wider offer surface than zoos / hotels / ski (3 candidates each) because casino economics support four distinct hypotheses and the user-chosen breadth at brainstorm maximizes outbound-angle test surface. ### Offer A — Pilot Zone (attach-rate-anchored, tactical complement)

**One-line statement.** Brite installs a single-zone programmable-media demonstration at an F&B or retail-promenade zone that already has an incumbent holiday-lighting or AV-installer vendor, underwriting the install cost in exchange for measured carded-player attach-rate lift, F&B per-cap uplift in the pilot zone, and case-study usage rights including property name, attach-rate delta, and footage.

**Hormozi value-equation mapping.**
- Dream Outcome: measurable carded-player attach-rate lift in the pilot zone the Director of F&B can defend to GM at quarterly review.
- Perceived Likelihood: high on narrow scope — single F&B patio / pool-deck / retail-arcade zone, one season, bounded failure mode.
- Time Delay: low (install in 4–8 weeks, results measurable within one operating month).
- Effort + Sacrifice: very low (single-zone scope, Brite handles everything, property contributes site access + carded-player attach measurement partnership).

**Frontend deliverable.** A rendered concept board for a named pilot zone (property-specific, e.g., "Pool Deck at Pechanga Cove" or "Porte-Cochère at Monarch Black Hawk" or "Retail Promenade at Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood"), with layout, programmable-media run-time schedule, carded-player-attach measurement plan, and a named baseline (prior-season or non-programmed-zone comparison).

**Backend commitment.** Pilot-zone install with a **casino-native guarantee metric** — carded-player attach rate in the pilot zone during programming hours vs. baseline, F&B per-cap lift in pilot-adjacent F&B operations, or loyalty-tier conversion rate on pilot-zone guests. Not photo-share metrics (gimmicky, disconnected from non-gaming scoreboard) and not gaming-floor-proximal metrics (regulated, out-of-zone).

**Commercial structure.** Brite underwrites install cost in exchange for case-study usage rights — footage, metrics, property name. Phrased confidently, not supplicantly: "We underwrite the install. In exchange, we use the results — attach-rate data, loyalty-conversion math, property name — in our sales material." (Per BC-5879 zoos-ledger Offer A fix #3 discipline.)

**When it fits.** Property has a long-tenured incumbent holiday-lighting or AV-installer vendor AND a decision-maker (typically Director of F&B or property GM) open to a low-commitment test but won't move on a full-property replacement from a cold-outbound touch. Strong fit at racinos (non-gaming footprint typically narrower) and at individual regional-commercial property GMs (single-property pilot logic).

### Offer B — Retention Infrastructure Subscription (retention-anchored, primary for year-round loyalty-integrated properties)

**One-line statement.** Year-round programmable-media architectural infrastructure tied to player-loyalty-tier promotions and daily / weekly content refresh cycles — subscription-model install, multi-year term, Brite retains infrastructure ownership, property licenses year-round use, loyalty-tier integration is the unit of analysis.

**Hormozi value-equation mapping (Dream Outcome + Perceived Likelihood dominant).**
- Dream Outcome: year-round architectural upgrade + amortized seasonal programming capacity that compounds the player-loyalty-tier acquisition funnel. Hard Rock Hollywood's Unity-tier $35 vs. general-admission pricing is the concrete dream-outcome shape.
- Perceived Likelihood: moderate — novel for the in-scope tier; [Hard Rock Hollywood "Hard Rock the Holidays"](https://casino.hardrock.com/hollywood/events/hard-rock-the-holidays) is the single strongest public precedent for the loyalty-tier-integrated programmable-media pattern.
- Time Delay: high (capital / subscription cycle 12–24 months, installation 8–16 weeks).
- Effort + Sacrifice: moderate-to-high (multi-year contract commitment, player-loyalty-program integration, cross-department coordination among Casino Operations + F&B + loyalty-program owner).

**Frontend deliverable.** An architectural-site-survey + year-round programmable-media design document with the loyalty-tier promotion calendar mapped (daily / weekly / seasonal content refresh cadence + compression-window amplification plan + F&B-concept-thematic overlay schedule).

**Backend commitment.** Multi-year subscription install with loyalty-program telemetry integration + year-round content refresh operation. Brite retains infrastructure ownership; property licenses year-round use. Revenue-share option: layer on top of subscription base fee, tied to incremental loyalty-tier conversion above a named baseline.

**When it fits.** Property has a mature player-loyalty program (Unity by Hard Rock, Caesars Rewards, M life Rewards at enterprise / mid-tier; tribal-enterprise equivalent at Yaamava', Choctaw, Foxwoods), a capital-planning window, and a clear zone (hotel tower exterior, pool deck, porte-cochere) ready for permanent programmable-media infrastructure. Strongest fit at Tribal Class III + regional-commercial-with-loyalty-integration tier. **Hard Rock Hollywood "Hard Rock the Holidays" is the ceiling precedent** — productize the pattern for the in-scope tier.

### Offer E — Production Finance (visitor-per-trip-anchored, primary for multi-stream properties)

**One-line statement.** Brite orchestrates multi-stream funding — tribal economic-development partnership grants + state tourism-bureau partnerships + F&B tenant co-marketing contribution + native ad revenue on programmable-media surfaces + optional player-loyalty-program co-investment — designs the programmable-media composition, runs the programming, and takes revenue-share on incremental visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue above a named baseline. **Hormozi value-equation mapping (Time Delay + Effort & Sacrifice dominant).**
- Dream Outcome: non-gaming programming that makes the property money net-of-install (not costs it) — visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue lift + F&B tenant retention + ad-revenue stream + loyalty-conversion funnel-fill all built in. Hedges the non-gaming-capex risk that sinks most cold-outbound vendor pitches.

- Time Delay: 9–12 months property-commit to program-live (longer than at zoos / ski because multi-stream close at tribal-governance-gate tier takes longer).
- Effort + Sacrifice: property near-zero capital, near-zero execution risk, operations limited to hosting + permits + F&B-tenant introductions.

**Frontend deliverable.** A production-finance deck with: named tribal-grant or state tourism-bureau partnership letter of intent (where applicable), ≥1 F&B tenant co-marketing commitment, regional-sponsor pledge or native-ad-revenue pre-commit, and Year-1 pro-forma revenue model (visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue delta + F&B attach lift + ad revenue + loyalty conversion + sponsor + grant line items, with a comp-budget-offset stress case demonstrating the non-gaming-ROI hedge). The deck itself IS the proof Brite has done the multi-party close work, not just the design work.

**Backend commitment.** Full-property non-gaming programming installation + multi-season operation. Brite finances against pipeline revenue. Property contributes site access, permits, operations hosting, F&B tenant introductions. Revenue-share on incremental visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue over a defined baseline (prior-year same-compression-window attach-and-per-cap + optional carded-player-segment baseline).

**Tech stack anchor.** DMX-pixel programmable-media on hotel tower + pool deck + porte-cochere + F&B patio + retail promenade (sourced from S4 Lights or equivalent component supplier). **Why this beats A / B / F against Moment Factory at Strip flagships.** Capital from Brite near-zero (removes the non-gaming-capex question that stalls outbound at in-scope-tier buyers). Property gets net-positive-revenue partnership, not expense line. ### Offer F — Comp-Multiplier (comp-rate-reduction-anchored, casino-native novel offer)

**One-line statement.** Revenue-share on measurable comp-budget-offset: Brite installs programmable-media; carded-player loyalty-tier telemetry tracks comp-rate reduction attributable to the non-gaming programming; Brite's fee is a negotiated % of the comp-budget savings. **New offer type not in shared [offer-design-frameworks.md](../offer-design-frameworks.md)** — documented inline per BC-5921 task-3 precedent (vertical-unique offer type added to playbook without upstreaming until productized).

**Hormozi value-equation mapping (Perceived Likelihood thin; Effort & Sacrifice very low).**
- Dream Outcome: non-gaming programming that directly pays for itself via comp-budget reduction — the offer aligns Brite's incentive with the casino's most operator-sensitive cost line.
- Perceived Likelihood: **low to moderate** — no public operator precedent for comp-rate reduction tied to experiential programming. R-6 research confirmed this is **empty narrative space with no verified public case study**. Brite has runway to define the frame; buyers will require a measurement-infrastructure proof before signing.
- Time Delay: moderate (measurement-infrastructure integration 3–6 months; comp-rate-delta measurement window 6–12 months).
- Effort + Sacrifice: very low for the property (Brite carries install + measurement integration; fee is a % of savings, not a cost line).

**Frontend deliverable.** A comp-multiplier measurement-infrastructure proposal: programmable-media zone scope + carded-player-loyalty-telemetry integration plan + comp-rate-delta measurement methodology (named baseline, carded-player-segment cohort, programming-hours attribution window) + Brite's fee-share-on-savings structure.

**Backend commitment.** Installation + player-loyalty-telemetry integration + shared measurement infrastructure + quarterly comp-rate-delta reporting. **Why Offer F is flagged pending productization.** Measurement infrastructure requires player-loyalty-program telemetry access (technical partnership with property's loyalty-program vendor) + shared attribution methodology (legal / accounting review). Revenue-share-on-comp-savings structure requires legal and accounting validation not yet completed. **Playbook surfaces Offer F as a candidate only; productization / legal / revenue-share accounting structure is separate work tracked outside this playbook.** Do not compose Offer F presets until productization lands.

**When it might fit (post-productization).** Regional-commercial operators with transparent comp-budget accounting (publicly traded, public 10-K comp disclosure) + GM / CFO ready for an operator-sensitive-data partnership + Brite has a reference-property comp-rate-delta precedent. Empty-narrative-space note: the first property is the hardest — no reference precedent yet exists.

## Voice rules

Six voice disciplines cumulative from BC-5930 issue body + R-6 research + BC-5920 / BC-5921 / BC-5922 transferable operator confirmations. All six apply to outbound email composition, account-research note-taking, and creative-angle generation consuming this playbook.

1. **Don't pitch gaming-floor, pit, count-room, cage, or compliance-adjacent lighting.** Brite's zone is the non-gaming footprint outside the NIGC / state-gaming-commission regulated perimeter — pool decks, porte-cocheres, F&B patios, retail promenades, hotel tower exteriors, boardwalk facades, exterior plazas. Any framing that proposes programmable-media inside the regulated perimeter reads as vendor fundamentally mis-understanding the compliance surface and collapses trust instantly. Target non-gaming zones; pitch Director of F&B / Casino GM / Regional VP Operations; stay outside the gaming perimeter in body copy and in every vendor-deck example. (Source: BC-5930 issue body + R-6 compliance research.)

2. **Don't pitch Marketing or Facilities as primary.** Marketing sells comp offers and loyalty-tier promotions, not non-gaming infrastructure; Facilities buys capex energy-retrofit, not non-gaming retention programming. The non-gaming decision surface is sub-type-dependent: GM + VP Casino Operations (regional commercial), Casino GM + Executive Officer Gaming & Hospitality + Tribal Council (tribal Class III), Director of Casino Operations (racinos), Regional VP of Operations (riverboat / multi-property). Director of F&B is shared secondary across all four. (Source: BC-5930 issue body + R-6 title-prevalence research.)

3. **Don't anchor US casino outbound on Strip or Boardwalk flagships as aspirational analogs.** Wynn, MGM, Bellagio, Caesars Palace, Venetian, Resorts World Las Vegas, Tropicana Atlantic City are ceiling references only — name them in the same sentence as a specific in-scope anchor (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood, Foxwoods, Yaamava', Choctaw, Monarch Black Hawk, Chamonix, Yonkers) framed as "the [flagship] pattern, productized for your scale and your carded-player program." Never as standalone framing. Growth is at regional + tribal + racino + riverboat tiers; the ICP excludes Strip / Boardwalk flagships. (Source: BC-5930 issue body + R-6 research.)

4. **Use operator / loyalty-program vocabulary, not hospitality-generalist or consumer-casino vocabulary.** Operator terms (non-gaming revenue share, carded-player attach, visitor-per-trip, comp budget, loyalty-tier pricing, per-cap, compression week, Unity tier / Caesars Rewards / M life tier, NIGC compact, Tribal Council budget cycle) signal Brite has done the homework. Hospitality-generalist terms (RevPAR, ADR) are out of vocabulary — casino P&L runs differently. Consumer-casino terms (high roller, whale, VIP suite, comp-hound) signal vendor trying too hard and erode credibility the rest of the email is building. (Source: R-6 trade-press vocabulary audit.)

5. **Lead with economic outcome, not aesthetic outcome.** Carded-player attach lift, loyalty-tier conversion, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue delta, comp-budget efficiency, F&B per-cap uplift, compression-week premium — not "spectacular lighting," "stunning programmable displays," "transforming the guest experience." Economic outcome is the Casino GM / VP Casino Operations / Director of F&B / Tribal Council / Regional VP vocabulary; aesthetic outcome is vendor-speak that signals Brite hasn't read the non-gaming P&L scoreboard. (Source: BC-5920 voice rule #5 generalized; R-6 confirms at casinos.)

6. **Respect tribal sovereignty in framing and in commercial structure.** At Tribal Class III prospects: address the tribe as a sovereign (not as a private commercial buyer); expect a Tribal Council or Tribal Gaming Commission procurement gate in addition to property-level approval; favor tribally-owned or 8(a) / Buy-Indian-Act-eligible subcontracting where applicable; procurement contracts should safeguard data ownership and limit sovereignty waivers ([Harvard Undergraduate Law Review 2025](https://hulr.org/fall-2025/reclaiming-authority-the-case-for-tribal-economic-sovereignty-in-the-gaming-era)). Reference sovereignty without over-claiming expertise; "we've read the sovereignty-aware procurement guidance and we're structuring this to be council-ready" is appropriate; "we're experts in tribal procurement" is not. (Source: R-6 tribal-sovereignty research + BC-5930 issue directive.)

## Anti-slop rules

Six rules casino-specific. Rule 1 is the one place the banned compliance-adjacent phrases appear in-text — the "don't" bullet format is the only section allowed to quote them. Voice rules and prose elsewhere describe the discipline without quoting.

1. **No Strip / Boardwalk flagship name-drops in framing or body copy.** Wynn, MGM, Bellagio, Caesars Palace, Venetian, Resorts World Las Vegas, Tropicana Atlantic City — these register as wrong-tier and wrong-scale to a regional / tribal / racino / riverboat GM or Director of F&B. (Appear in this rule bullet and in § Vendor landscape ceiling-reference citations only; never in standalone framing or outbound hook.)

2. **No "luxury" / "opulent" / "high-roller-only" / "VIP-only" buzzwords.** Consumer-casino-marketing warm-glow language that zeroes out credibility on a non-gaming-economics buyer. If the programming matters, the numbers describe it (attach lift, loyalty conversion, per-cap delta, comp efficiency). If the numbers aren't there, adjectives don't substitute. (Source: BC-5930 issue directive — "no 'luxury' / 'opulent' buzzwords" — extended to cover the consumer-casino vocabulary surface.)

3. **No pit / gaming-floor / count-room / cage / surveillance language.** Compliance-adjacent framing collapses trust instantly — Brite's zone is explicitly OUTSIDE the NIGC / state-commission regulated perimeter. Outbound that proposes programmable-media inside the regulated perimeter reads as fundamentally mis-understanding the compliance surface. "Gaming floor," "pit," "table games," "slot arrays," "count room," "cage," "surveillance" are non-vocabulary in body copy. (Source: BC-5930 issue body + R-6 compliance research.)

4. **No "experience upgrade" standalone buzzword.** Rounds off to vendor-speak that doesn't name the specific non-gaming-economic outcome; the Director of F&B / VP Casino Operations buyer reads "experience upgrade" as consultant-filler. Use the specific metric (attach lift, loyalty conversion, comp-budget efficiency) the buyer tracks. (Source: BC-5930 issue directive + cross-vertical anti-slop discipline.)

5. **No generic "ROI uplift" without a named casino-specific metric.** Casino economics are specific — carded-player attach rate, loyalty-tier conversion delta, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue, comp-budget efficiency, F&B per-cap, compression-week premium.
