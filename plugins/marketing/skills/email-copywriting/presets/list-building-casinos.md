## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't pitch gaming-floor, pit, table games, slot arrays, count room, cage, or surveillance programmable-media. Brite's zone is the non-gaming footprint outside the NIGC and state-gaming-commission regulated perimeter. Outbound that proposes programmable-media inside the regulated perimeter reads as vendor fundamentally mis-understanding the compliance surface and collapses trust instantly.
- Don't pitch Marketing or Facilities as primary. Marketing sells comp offers and loyalty-tier promotions; Facilities buys capex energy-retrofits. The non-gaming decision surface is sub-type-dependent: GM + VP Casino Operations (regional commercial), Casino GM + Executive Officer Gaming and Hospitality + Tribal Council (tribal Class III), Director of Casino Operations (racinos), Regional VP of Operations (riverboat or multi-property). Director of F and B is shared secondary across all four.
- Don't anchor on Las Vegas Strip or Atlantic City Boardwalk flagships as standalone framing. Wynn, MGM, Bellagio, Caesars Palace, Venetian, Resorts World Las Vegas, Tropicana Atlantic City are ceiling references only, named paired with a specific in-scope anchor (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood, Foxwoods, Yaamava, Choctaw, Monarch Black Hawk, Chamonix, Yonkers) in the same sentence. Growth is at regional commercial, tribal Class III, racino, and riverboat tiers; the ICP excludes Strip and Boardwalk flagships.
- No "luxury", "opulent", "high-roller-only", or "VIP-only" buzzwords. Consumer-casino warm-glow vocabulary zeroes out credibility on a non-gaming-economics buyer. If the programming matters, the numbers describe it (carded-player attach lift, loyalty-tier conversion, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue, F and B per-cap delta, comp-budget efficiency). If the numbers aren't there, adjectives don't substitute.
- Use operator and loyalty-program vocabulary, not hospitality-generalist or consumer-casino vocabulary. Operator terms (carded-player attach, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue, loyalty-tier conversion, compression week, comp budget, Unity-tier or Caesars Rewards or M life tier, NIGC compact, Tribal Council budget cycle) signal Brite has done the homework. Hospitality-generalist terms (RevPAR, ADR) are out of vocabulary. Consumer-casino terms (high roller, whale, VIP suite, comp-hound) erode credibility.
- No "experience upgrade" standalone buzzword. Rounds off to vendor-speak that doesn't name the specific non-gaming-economic outcome. Use the specific metric (attach lift, loyalty conversion, comp-budget efficiency) the Director of F and B or VP Casino Operations tracks.
- No generic "ROI uplift" without a named casino-specific metric. Casino economics are specific: carded-player attach rate, loyalty-tier conversion delta, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue, comp-budget efficiency, F and B per-cap, compression-week premium. Generic phrasing signals vendor hasn't done casino-vertical homework.
- Lead with non-gaming economic outcome; tribal-sovereignty, destination-branding, and press-worthy legacy live in paragraph 2 or later, not the hook. Opening cold outbound with sovereignty-expertise or destination-branding narrative reads as over-claiming or agency-speak to a Casino GM or Director of F and B measured on non-gaming scoreboard at quarterly review.
- Respect tribal sovereignty in framing at Tribal Class III prospects. Address the tribe as a sovereign; expect a Tribal Council or Tribal Gaming Commission procurement gate in addition to property-level approval; reference sovereignty without over-claiming expertise. "We've read the sovereignty-aware procurement guidance and we're structuring this to be Council-ready" is appropriate; "we're experts in tribal procurement" is not.
- Frame Moment Factory as ceiling reference only, not primary competitor at in-scope tier. Resorts World Las Vegas GLOW and Tropicana Atlantic City boardwalk are Strip and Boardwalk flagships, well above in-scope-tier capex norms; Brite's Offer E is the productized pattern for the tier below Moment Factory's engagement floor. Name Moment Factory only paired with an in-scope anchor, never standalone.
- Frame MK Illumination as adjacent-not-competitive at in-scope tier. MK's one US casino-adjacent install is a Strip retail-corridor. Active-watch at attached-shopping-corridor build-outs; do not frame as direct competitor at the regional or tribal tier.
- Frame S4 Lights as Brite's partner, never a competitor. S4 is the DMX-pixel component supplier whose venue portfolio Brite name-drops for breadth credibility and whose supply-chain secures the pixel hardware.
- Don't use urgency tactics. Casino capital cycles run through Tribal Councils, capital committees, and regional VPs on multi-month and multi-year cadence; urgency reads as vendor-desperate and under-researched.
- Don't cite Brite Nites seasonal residential case studies. Cite Labs experiential or capital installations only (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood as ceiling-in-scope anchor, Choctaw 23-property digital-signage portfolio, S4 venue portfolio). Residential analogs misread the Offer E motion and read as scale-mismatched.
- Don't reference installers or property management. Supply-excluded per handbook canon.
