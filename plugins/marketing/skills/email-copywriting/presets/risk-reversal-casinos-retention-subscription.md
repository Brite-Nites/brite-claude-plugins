## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't pitch gaming-floor, pit, table games, slot arrays, count room, cage, or surveillance programmable-media. Brite's zone is the non-gaming footprint outside the NIGC and state-gaming-commission regulated perimeter. The retention-subscription motion reads loyalty-program telemetry from the property's existing system; Brite does not touch the regulated player-data layer.
- Don't pitch Marketing or Facilities as primary. The retention-subscription decision surface is sub-type-dependent: GM + VP Casino Operations (regional commercial), Casino GM + Executive Officer Gaming and Hospitality + Tribal Council (tribal Class III), Director of Casino Operations (racinos), Regional VP of Operations (riverboat or multi-property). Director of F and B is shared secondary.
- Don't anchor on Las Vegas Strip or Atlantic City Boardwalk flagships as standalone framing. Strip integrated-resort loyalty programs run at scale and capital norms above in-scope-tier; ceiling references only, paired with in-scope anchors (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood, Foxwoods, Yaamava, Choctaw, Monarch Black Hawk).
- No "luxury", "opulent", "high-roller-only", or "VIP-only" buzzwords. Capital committees and Tribal Councils evaluating a multi-year subscription price conversion math, not warm-glow adjectives.
- Don't over-promise loyalty-tier conversion metrics beyond Labs' defensible threshold. The guarantee is credible only if Labs can defend the specific conversion-rate threshold with real data (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood ceiling-in-scope precedent, S4 venue portfolio, and the shared retention-subscription motion).
- Avoid guarantee language that triggers casino finance, ownership legal, or Tribal Gaming Commission review: "refund in full", "zero-cost until delivered", "money-back". Use outcome-gated framing: "fee not billed until conversion clears baseline".
- No "experience upgrade" standalone buzzword. The T4 tone especially requires naming the specific loyalty-tier metric the carded-player program tracks.
- No generic "ROI uplift" without a named loyalty-tier-specific metric. T4 tone rewards specificity: loyalty-tier conversion rate delta, carded-player attach rate lift, Unity-tier or peer-tier differential adoption, year-round architectural amortization ratio, year-two renewal upside.
- Don't name competitors in body copy (Moment Factory, MK Illumination, Tianyu, Christie Digital, Limelight Art, CCS, Nanolumens, HB Communications). T4 is Brite's own commitment.

Reference the Tribal Council capex-gate without over-claiming expertise; procurement contracts should safeguard data ownership and limit sovereignty waivers; favor tribally-owned or 8(a) or Buy-Indian-Act-eligible subcontracting where applicable.
- Don't use urgency tactics. Multi-year subscription contracts with loyalty-program-telemetry integration run through capital committees, Tribal Councils, and regional portfolios on multi-month cycles.

- Lead with loyalty-tier conversion or carded-player attach economic outcome; tribal-sovereignty, destination-branding, and press-worthy legacy live in paragraph 2 or later, not the hook.
