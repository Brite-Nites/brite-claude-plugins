# list-building | Hotels & Resorts | Brite Labs

## Hook (vertical-specific recency waterfall)

Anchor on the capital-window signal: new GM appointment (the 6-12 month audit cycle is the outbound window), renovation or F&B concept press, capital bond or ownership-group announcement, or a comp-set under-index on ADR in the most recent quarterly STR trend. Template hook: "Saw {PROPERTY_SIGNAL} land on {DATE_SIGNAL}." Avoid generic "holiday season" openers (read vendor-desperate). {GM_NAME} or {DOR_NAME} anchors the greeting-merged first sentence; default to GM at boutique and smaller regional properties where the Revenue Manager role folds into GM. Package-launch press decays at 3-6 months and is the better anchor for Offer A tactical; do not use it here since Offer E is the green-field program build.

## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't pitch "lighting upgrade" or "holiday install" framing. That vocabulary routes the email to engineering / facilities buyers on capex energy-reduction, not to the revenue-manager scorecard. Pitch named-seasonal-program economics with ADR lift, direct-channel mix, and F&B / spa attach as the measurable levers.
- Don't name-drop Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, Ritz-Carlton, Four Seasons, or Waldorf Astoria as peer anchors. Enterprise flagships are wrong-scale and wrong-procurement for boutique and independent ICP. Use Fairmont Scottsdale Princess (15-year YoY continuity), Hotel del Coronado (1904 heritage plus annual theme rotation), Callaway Resort & Gardens (Lodge package economics), or Biltmore Inn as peer proof points.
- Don't address "VP Revenue Management" or "VP of Experience" in the salutation. Those titles do not exist at 100-400 key independents. Pitch GM as primary, Director of Revenue as co-primary where the role exists, DOSM as influencer, Owner-Operator as primary at family-owned and private-ownership properties.
- Don't use "romantic", "ambiance", "magical", "enchanting", "dazzling", or "mesmerizing" in framing or voice. That is guest-facing brochure vocabulary and signals someone who writes marketing copy, not someone who moves revenue.
- Don't lead with wedding-venue framing. Wedding-revenue positioning routes the email to Events Sales and dies there. If wedding revenue surfaces at all, it belongs in paragraph 2+ as an attach layer behind the seasonal-program hook.
- Don't use urgency language ("limited availability", "book now", "only N slots left"). Hotel GMs and Directors of Revenue field dozens of vendor pitches and filter on low-trust vendor-sales-manual tactics first. Urgency is earned by genuine compression-window timing, not manufactured in copy.
- Don't use generic "ROI uplift" phrasing. Hotel economics are specific: peak-weekend ADR lift, RevPAR trajectory on compression dates, direct-channel mix shift, length-of-stay minimums on program weekends, F&B and spa attach rate on package guests. Generic "ROI" signals the vendor did not do hotel-vertical homework.
