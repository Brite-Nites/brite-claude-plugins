# list-building | Zoos | Brite Labs (pilot zone)

## Hook (vertical-specific recency waterfall)

Anchor on a recency signal that suggests the incumbent program has a bounded blind spot: an announced new exhibit without lighting scope, a zone refresh that the existing program didn't touch, a sponsor-visibility gap on one attraction, or a Marketing or Corporate-Partnerships role change within 90 days. Tone: conversational and specific, not pitching a replacement. {FIRST_NAME} anchors the greeting-merged first sentence. Template: "{saw|noticed} {COMPANY}'s {ZONE_OR_SIGNAL}".

## Step 1 skeleton

Base skeleton A adaptation tuned for Offer A (pilot-zone tactical complement). The three Offer A fixes from the ledger are load-bearing: frontend deliverable is a rendered concept board (tangible artifact, not a visualization promise); commercial structure is confident underwrite (not supplicant case-study ask); incumbent framing stays general ("existing lighting vendor"). `{PILOT_ZONE_AREA}` is operator-filled with a specific zone at the venue (e.g., "the new flamingo habitat plaza"). `{LABS_PEER_VENUE}` defaults to Hogle Zoo; `{S4_PARTNER}` fills as "S4 Lights".

## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't frame the pilot as a replacement of the incumbent. Frame as a "specialist add-on" or "complementary zone". Positioning matters for the decision-maker's internal politics with the existing vendor.
- Don't over-specify incumbent vendor type in body copy. Use "existing lighting vendor", "incumbent", or "current seasonal-program vendor", never "lantern vendor", "projection vendor", or any sub-category shorthand.
- Don't name-drop Disney, Universal, or theme-park analogs. Stay within zoo-precedent anchors: Lincoln Park ZooLights, Columbus Zoo, Nashville Zoolumination, Hogle Zoo.
- Use "program" not "event" or "activation". Zoos run multi-season programs with YoY refresh logic.
- Don't name species in prose (tigers, elephants, giraffes). Use "your collection", "your flagship exhibits", or the specific zone name.
- Frame the commercial structure confidently, not supplicantly. Brite underwrites the install cost; the case-study usage rights are the exchange, not a favor being asked.
- The deliverable is a rendered concept board (a tangible 24"×36" artifact), not a "visualization" or "3D walkthrough". Tangible artifact only, per BC-5879 ledger § Offer A fix #1.
- No generic "ROI uplift" without a named metric. Pilot-zone economics are specific: dwell time in the zone, attendance uplift in programmed hours, or sponsor-visibility impressions.
- No "magical", "enchanting", "wondrous", or "mesmerizing" adjectives. Consultant-speak reads as the wrong vendor type.
- Don't use urgency tactics. Pilot-zone decisions run through Marketing and Corporate Partnerships on their own cadence; urgency signals desperation and disrespects the incumbent relationship.
- Frame S4 Lights as Brite's partner, not a competitor. S4 is the DMX-pixel component supplier whose supply-chain credibility underwrites the pilot-zone hardware.
- Don't reference installers or property management. Supply-excluded per handbook canon.
- Conservation and mission framing surface as supporting beats in paragraph 2 or later, never as the hook. Pilot-zone conversations open on the specific zone and the concept board, not on the zoo's mission.
