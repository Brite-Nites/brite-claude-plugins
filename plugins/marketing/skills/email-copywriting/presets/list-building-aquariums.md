# list-building | Aquariums | Brite Labs

## Hook (vertical-specific recency waterfall)

Anchor on the strongest public signal. Top of the waterfall: a named title-sponsor pledge (GEICO / YuleTides, UPMC / Wild Illuminations, Abita / Scales & Ales precedent), a published economic-impact figure (National Aquarium Baltimore's $430M statewide economic activity + 3,400 jobs is the national-scale anchor; SC Aquarium $378M and Great Lakes Aquarium $30M are mid-market anchors), or a new Executive Director / CEO / Director of Marketing & Admissions Revenue / Director of Corporate Partnerships role change within 90 days.

Second tier: ticket-on-sale announcement for a 21+ program ($39.95-$59.99 adult range, Newport After Dark $59.99 and Aquarium of the Pacific Night Dive $44.95 as the price-point anchors), a program announcement for summer Glow Nights / Night Dive / Aquarium After Dark / Christmas Underwater, or a capital-campaign press release. Greeting-merges into the first sentence. Template: "{saw|noticed} {COMPANY}'s {RECENCY_ANCHOR}". Open with revenue-diversification outcome; mission and conservation framing lives in paragraph 2, not the hook (per BC-5920 zoos gate, carried forward).

## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't name-drop enterprise-flagship aquariums as body-copy analogs. Georgia Aquarium, Monterey Bay Aquarium, Shedd Aquarium, New England Aquarium, Seattle Aquarium register as wrong-scale and wrong-buyer to a mid-market independent Executive Director; architectural-lighting-design firms (Lightswitch, Fisher Marantz Stone, CD+M, HLB, Available Light) own the flagship capital relationship. Stay within mid-market anchors: Newport Aquarium KY, Aquarium of the Pacific (Long Beach, CA, not Newport Beach), Adventure Aquarium NJ, Oregon Coast, SC Aquarium, Audubon New Orleans, Virginia Aquarium, Maritime Aquarium at Norwalk.
- Don't address "VP Revenue" or "Director of Adult Programs" in salutations. Mid-market / independent aquariums run Executive Director / President & CEO (often dual-hatted as "VP & Executive Director" per Newport KY + Adventure NJ pattern), Director of Marketing & Admissions Revenue, Director of Corporate Partnerships, Director of Development, Director of Operations, Director of Guest Experience. VP-titled Revenue roles are enterprise-only; "Director of Adult Programs" does not exist at mid-market.
- Don't pitch tank-interior or husbandry-controlled lighting (chroma-match coral, circadian-match jelly tanks, species-specific wavelength tuning). Brite's zone is architectural-gallery overlay only; tank lighting is controlled by aquarium staff and veterinary advisors. This is a hard compliance line, not a preference.
- Don't use consumer-marketing adjectives that aquariums use in their own ticket-buyer copy: "magical undersea", "immersive wonder", "dive into", "dazzling", "mesmerizing", "enchanting", "shimmering", "breathtaking", "captivating depths", "ocean magic", "sea of wonder", "under the sea". A vendor lifting that vocabulary signals someone who writes brochures, not someone who moves revenue.
- Don't name species in prose (sharks, jellies, octopuses, seahorses, specific tank names). Use "your collection", "your flagship gallery", "your tank galleries", or the specific public-program name if the aquarium has one.
- Don't use "dive-into", "sea of", "tides of", or "swimming in" metaphors. Replace with operator-native verbs: install, program, underwrite, measure, amortize.
- Use aquarium-native content vocabulary: bioluminescence, UV-reactive overlay, jelly-gallery wash, kelp-forest gallery, reef-gallery facade, architectural lobby wash, gallery-ceiling overlay, mezzanine programmable-media. These are the phrasings operators use with their boards.
- No generic "ROI uplift" without a named aquarium metric. Aquarium economics are specific: 21+ ticket revenue, F&B or bar attach per-cap, membership conversion, regional economic-impact share, sponsor-inventory sell-through. Generic "ROI" signals the vendor didn't do aquarium-vertical homework.
- Frame S4 Lights as Brite's partner, not a competitor. S4 is the DMX-pixel component supplier whose venue-portfolio breadth credentials the architectural-gallery hardware.
- Don't cite Brite Nites seasonal residential case studies. Cite Labs experiential or capital installations only; residential analogs misread the architectural-commercial motion.
- Don't frame Moment Factory, MK Illumination, or Tianyu Arts & Culture as Brite competitors at US aquariums. Moment Factory has zero named US aquarium deployments (the OdySea Aquarium 360° lobby was Malvern Entertainment + Christie Digital + CCS). MK's US portfolio has zero aquarium work. Tianyu is mechanically excluded (humidity, footprint, portfolio evidence).
- Don't use urgency tactics. Aquarium procurement runs through Executive Director / CEO on multi-month cycles; urgency signals vendor-desperate.
- Conservation and mission framing surface in paragraph 2 or later, never as the hook. Opening with mission-speak from a vendor reads as performative to an Executive Director or Director of Corporate Partnerships.
