# list-building | Casinos | Brite Labs (pilot zone)

## Hook (vertical-specific recency waterfall)

Anchor on a recency signal that suggests the incumbent holiday-lighting or AV-installer vendor has a bounded blind spot. Top of the waterfall: a new F and B concept launch without integrated programmable-media scope, a pool deck or porte-cochere refresh the incumbent didn't touch, a single-outlet sponsor-visibility gap, or a Director of F and B role change within 90 days. Second tier: a retail-promenade refresh announcement where the scope didn't include programmable lighting, a boardwalk-facade announcement at a racino or riverboat property, or a Casino GM appointment at a property with known incumbent-vendor lock-in.

Tone: conversational and specific, not pitching a replacement. The incumbent relationship stays clean and the pilot is framed as a specialist add-on. {FIRST_NAME} anchors the greeting-merged first sentence. Template: `"{saw|noticed} {COMPANY}'s {ZONE_OR_SIGNAL}"`.

## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't frame the pilot as a replacement of the incumbent AV-integrator or holiday-lighting vendor. Frame as a "specialist add-on" or "complementary zone". Positioning matters for the decision-maker's internal politics with the existing vendor relationship.
- Don't over-specify incumbent vendor type in body copy. Use "existing AV-integrator", "incumbent", "current holiday-lighting vendor", or "existing full-property vendor". Never use "lantern vendor", "projection vendor", "turnkey AV installer", or any sub-category shorthand surfaced from the vendor-landscape research.
- The deliverable is a rendered concept board (a tangible 24"×36" artifact), not a "visualization", "3D walkthrough", or "simulation". Tangible artifact only, per BC-5879 ledger § Offer A fix #1.
- Don't pitch gaming-floor, pit, table games, slot arrays, count room, cage, or surveillance programmable-media. Brite's zone is the non-gaming footprint. Pilot zones are non-gaming-zone-only: F and B patio, pool deck, porte-cochere, retail promenade, hotel tower exterior, boardwalk facade.
- Don't pitch Marketing or Facilities as primary. Pilot-zone decision-makers are Director of F and B (native P&L authority on F and B zone pilots) or property GM (native authority on capital-pilot decisions within delegated spending authority). Explicitly excluded per playbook.
- Don't anchor on Las Vegas Strip or Atlantic City Boardwalk flagships. Ceiling-only and paired with in-scope mid-market anchor (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood, Foxwoods, Yaamava, Choctaw, Pechanga, Monarch Black Hawk, Chamonix, Yonkers) in the same sentence.
- No "luxury", "opulent", "high-roller-only", or "VIP-only" buzzwords. Consumer-casino warm-glow vocabulary reads as the wrong vendor type on a Director of F and B evaluating attach-math on a specific zone.
- No "experience upgrade" standalone buzzword. The pilot is specific (one zone, one season, named metric); "experience upgrade" rounds off to vendor-speak.
- Frame the commercial structure confidently, not supplicantly. Brite underwrites the install cost; the case-study usage rights are the exchange, not a favor being asked (per BC-5879 ledger § Offer A fix #3).
- Pilot-zone guarantee metric is casino-native: carded-player attach rate in pilot zone during programming hours (measured through the property's existing carded-player program telemetry), F and B per-cap lift in pilot-adjacent F and B operations (POS delta vs prior-year baseline), or loyalty-tier conversion rate on pilot-zone guests (carded-player conversion from general admission to Unity tier or peer tier). Never photo-share or social-media-share (per BC-5879 ledger § Offer A fix #2). Never gaming-floor-proximal (regulated out-of-zone per playbook anti-slop rule 3).
- No generic "ROI uplift" without a named casino-specific metric. Pilot-zone economics are specific: carded-player attach-rate delta, F and B per-cap lift in pilot-adjacent operations, or loyalty-tier conversion delta on pilot-zone guests.
- Don't use urgency tactics. Pilot-zone decisions run through Director of F and B and property GM on their own cadence; urgency signals vendor desperation and disrespects the incumbent relationship.
- Frame S4 Lights as Brite's partner, not a competitor. S4 is the DMX-pixel component supplier whose supply-chain underwrites the pilot-zone hardware.
- Don't cite Brite Nites seasonal residential case studies. Cite Labs experiential or capital installations only (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood ceiling-in-scope anchor, S4 venue portfolio).
- Don't reference installers or property management. Supply-excluded per handbook canon.
- Respect tribal sovereignty in framing at Tribal Class III prospects, even at pilot-zone scope. A pilot-zone commercial structure at a tribal enterprise still routes through property-level Council-aware etiquette; "we're structuring the pilot to be Council-ready if the pilot earns expansion" is appropriate at tribal properties.
