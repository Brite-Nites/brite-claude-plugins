## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't pitch gaming-floor, pit, table games, slot arrays, count room, cage, or surveillance programmable-media. Brite's zone is the non-gaming footprint outside the NIGC and state-gaming-commission regulated perimeter.
- Don't pitch Marketing or Facilities as primary. The non-gaming decision surface is sub-type-dependent: GM + VP Casino Operations (regional commercial), Casino GM + Executive Officer Gaming and Hospitality + Tribal Council (tribal Class III), Director of Casino Operations (racinos), Regional VP of Operations (riverboat or multi-property). Director of F and B is shared secondary across all four. At 300M+ capital programs Council-safe or committee-safe framing is required.
- Don't anchor on Las Vegas Strip or Atlantic City Boardwalk flagships as standalone framing. Wynn, MGM, Bellagio, Caesars Palace, Venetian, Resorts World Las Vegas, Tropicana Atlantic City are ceiling references only, paired with a specific in-scope anchor (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood, Foxwoods, Yaamava, Choctaw, Monarch Black Hawk, Chamonix, Yonkers) in the same sentence.
- No "luxury", "opulent", "high-roller-only", or "VIP-only" buzzwords. Consumer-casino warm-glow vocabulary zeroes out credibility on a capital committee or Tribal Council.
- Don't over-promise performance metrics beyond Labs' documented case studies. The guarantee is credible only if Labs can defend the specific threshold with real data (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood ceiling-in-scope precedent, Choctaw 23-property digital-signage portfolio, S4 venue portfolio, and the shared production-finance motion).
- Avoid guarantee language that triggers casino finance, ownership legal, or Tribal Gaming Commission review: "refund in full", "zero-cost until delivered", "money-back". Use outcome-gated framing: "fee not billed until breakeven".
- No "experience upgrade" standalone buzzword. Use the specific metric (carded-player attach lift, loyalty-tier conversion, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue, comp-budget efficiency, F and B per-cap delta) the buyer tracks.
- No generic "ROI uplift" without a named casino-specific metric. T4 tone especially rewards specificity: carded-player attach rate, loyalty-tier conversion delta, visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue, comp-budget efficiency, F and B per-cap, compression-week premium.
- Don't name competitors in body copy (Moment Factory, MK Illumination, Tianyu, Christie Digital, Limelight Art, CCS, Nanolumens, HB Communications). T4 is Brite's own commitment, not a head-to-head pitch. Even implied comparisons reduce credibility on the ownership or Council review.
- Frame S4 Lights as Brite's partner, never a competitor. S4 is the DMX-pixel component supplier whose supply-chain underwrites hardware assurance on the guarantee.
- Respect tribal sovereignty in framing and commercial structure at Tribal Class III prospects. Reference the Tribal Council capex-gate without over-claiming expertise; favor tribally-owned or 8(a) or Buy-Indian-Act-eligible subcontracting where applicable; procurement contracts should safeguard data ownership and limit sovereignty waivers.
- Don't use urgency tactics. Tribal Councils, capital committees, and Regional VP portfolios approve non-gaming investments on multi-month or multi-year cycles; urgency reads as vendor-desperate.
- Don't cite Brite Nites seasonal residential case studies. Cite Labs experiential or capital installations only; residential analogs undercut the commercial T4 tone.
- Don't reference installers or property management. Supply-excluded per handbook canon.
- Lead with non-gaming economic outcome; tribal-sovereignty, destination-branding, and press-worthy legacy live in paragraph 2 or later, not the hook.
