## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't use vanity-metric guarantees (guest photo-shares, Instagram geotag counts, press mentions, social-engagement lifts). The guarantee metric must live on the Director of Revenue's scorecard: peak-weekend ADR lift, direct-channel mix shift, length-of-stay minimums on program weekends, or F&B / spa attach rate. Vanity metrics signal the vendor did not do hotel-vertical homework.
- Don't propose "photo-share guarantee" or "social-reach floor" language. Zoos-vertical voice that migrated in from early drafts; wrong scorecard for hotels.
- Don't frame the underwrite supplicantly ("we would love a chance to prove ourselves", "we would be grateful for the opportunity"). The voice is confident: "we underwrite the install, we use the results in our sales material, the trade is clean." Supplicant framing undercuts the guarantee credibility.
- Don't name-drop Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, Ritz-Carlton, Four Seasons, or Waldorf Astoria as guarantee precedents. Use Hotel del Coronado (annual theme rotation), Fairmont Scottsdale Princess (15-year compounding), or Callaway Resort & Gardens (package economics) as the proof surface. Enterprise flagships run a different procurement model and those anchors signal mis-read property scale.
- Don't address "VP Revenue Management" or "VP of Experience" in the salutation. Pitch Director of Revenue as primary, GM as sign-authority, Owner-Operator as primary at family-owned properties.
- Don't use "romantic", "ambiance", "magical", "enchanting", "dazzling", or "mesmerizing" in the guarantee framing. These are guest-brochure words and they instantly recode a T4 risk-reversal pitch as low-trust vendor copy to a revenue-manager audience.
- Don't lead with wedding-venue or wedding-photo guarantees. Wedding revenue routes to Events Sales and dies there; the pilot conversation lives on the revenue-manager scorecard.
- Don't use urgency tactics. The pilot is sized to the property's audit cycle, not to Brite's pipeline pressure. Urgency reads as a vendor-sales-manual tactic to a revenue manager.
- Don't propose guarantee structures that would require legal review beyond a standard vendor MSA amendment (e.g. "refund in full", "zero-cost until delivered", percent-of-revenue claims without a defined measurement window). The underwrite-the-install framing is the clean version of the guarantee and holds up without legal escalation.
