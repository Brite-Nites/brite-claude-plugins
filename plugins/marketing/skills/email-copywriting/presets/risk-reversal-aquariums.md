# risk-reversal | Aquariums | Brite Labs

## Hook (vertical-specific recency waterfall)

Anchor on the procurement-weight signal: a large named title sponsor already in pipeline (GEICO / YuleTides, UPMC / Wild Illuminations, Abita / Scales & Ales precedent to cite by name), a published economic-impact study (National Aquarium Baltimore's $430M statewide economic activity is the national ceiling; SC Aquarium $378M, Virginia Aquarium $250M, Great Lakes Aquarium $30M out-of-area spend are mid-market anchors), a city or CVB partnership with public dollars attached, or a multi-year capital-plan press release.

Tone shift from list-building T2: less conversational, more respectful of the multi-season commitment and the committee process. {FIRST_NAME} anchors the greeting-merged first sentence. The hook should read as if written by someone who understands that signing a multi-year programmable-media lease is a board-visible decision at a nonprofit-governed institution.

## Step 1 skeleton

Base skeleton B adaptation. Guarantee shape: first-season-breakeven-or-no-pay. The install fee is not billed until incremental 21+ ticket revenue over prior-year baseline clears the agreed floor at season close, measured from the aquarium's ticketing-system report (cleanest data source). Follows BC-5932 task-2 pattern, keyed to aquarium-native metric. `{LABS_PEER_VENUE}` defaults to Newport Aquarium KY or Aquarium of the Pacific (Long Beach, CA); `{S4_PARTNER}` fills as "S4 Lights" (supply-chain credibility, not competitor). `{BASELINE_TERMS}` is operator-filled at draft time and names the revenue streams counted toward breakeven (21+ ticket revenue, F&B and bar attach, membership-conversion uplift).

## Step 2 bump

Tone: specificity as credibility. The bump names which line items count toward breakeven, so the committee can evaluate the guarantee structure against real accounting rather than marketing language.

**Subject:** `Re: {Season guarantee|21+ underwrite|Break-even terms}`

**Body:**

{Following up|Circling back}, {FIRST_NAME}. A guarantee like this {takes time|deserves a real look|runs through committee}, which is fair. To be specific about breakeven: gate-scan 21+ ticket revenue over prior-year baseline, plus F&B and bar attach paid commitments on after-hours guests, plus membership-conversion uplift measured through the adult-program funnel, all tallied against the install fee at season close. If the math doesn't land, the fee isn't billed. That specificity is the point; it's what holds up under review.<br><br>{Best|Cheers|Thanks},<br>{SENDER_FIRST_NAME}

**wait_in_days:** 3

## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't name-drop enterprise-flagship aquariums as guarantee precedents. Georgia Aquarium, Monterey Bay Aquarium, Shedd Aquarium, New England Aquarium, Seattle Aquarium run architectural-lighting-design-firm capital relationships (Lightswitch Shedd Jellies, Fisher Marantz Stone Georgia, HLB Seattle Ocean Pavilion) on multi-year exhibit-refresh cycles; the flagship guarantee shape does not translate. Stay within mid-market anchors: Newport Aquarium KY, Aquarium of the Pacific (Long Beach, CA, not Newport Beach), Adventure Aquarium NJ, Oregon Coast, SC Aquarium, Audubon New Orleans, Virginia Aquarium, Maritime Aquarium at Norwalk.
- Don't use vanity-metric guarantees (guest photo-shares, Instagram geotag counts, press mentions, social-engagement lifts). The guarantee metric must live on the Director of Marketing & Admissions Revenue's scorecard: incremental 21+ ticket revenue over prior-year baseline, F&B or bar attach per-cap on after-hours guests, membership-conversion uplift from the adult-program funnel, or regional economic-impact share with a published peer anchor.
- Don't over-promise performance metrics beyond Labs' documented case studies. The guarantee is credible only if Labs can defend the specific outcome with real data.
- Avoid guarantee language that triggers nonprofit legal review ("refund in full", "zero-cost until delivered", "money-back"). Use outcome-gated framing: "fee not billed until breakeven".
- Don't pitch tank-interior or husbandry-controlled lighting. Brite's zone is architectural-gallery overlay only; tank lighting is controlled by aquarium staff and veterinary advisors. This is a hard compliance line, not a preference.
- Don't use consumer-marketing adjectives ("magical undersea", "immersive wonder", "dive into", "dazzling", "mesmerizing", "enchanting", "shimmering", "breathtaking", "captivating depths", "ocean magic", "sea of wonder", "under the sea"). Consumer-marketing adjectives zero out credibility on an economic-outcome committee.
- Don't name species in prose (sharks, jellies, octopuses, seahorses, specific tank names). Use "your collection", "your flagship gallery", "your tank galleries", or the specific public-program name.
- Don't use "dive-into", "sea of", "tides of", or "swimming in" metaphors on a T4 guarantee email. T4 tone especially rewards operator-native verbs: install, program, underwrite, measure, amortize.
- Don't address "VP Revenue" or "Director of Adult Programs" in salutations. Lead with Executive Director / CEO, Director of Corporate Partnerships, or Director of Marketing & Admissions Revenue. VP-titled Revenue roles are enterprise-only; "Director of Adult Programs" does not exist at mid-market.
- Don't cite Brite Nites seasonal residential case studies. Cite Labs experiential or capital installations only; residential analogs undercut the commercial T4 tone.
- Don't name competitors in body copy (Moment Factory, MK Illumination, Tianyu, Lightswitch, Fisher Marantz Stone, CD+M, HLB). T4 is Brite's own commitment, not a head-to-head pitch. Moment Factory also has zero named US aquarium deployments (the OdySea Aquarium 360° lobby was Malvern Entertainment + Christie Digital + CCS); any implied comparison mis-reads the landscape.
- Don't use urgency tactics. Capital and multi-season commitments at aquariums run through boards and development committees on multi-month cycles; urgency reads as vendor-desperate.
- Frame S4 Lights as Brite's partner, not a competitor. S4 is the DMX-pixel component supplier; T4 credibility leans on the supply-chain partnership for hardware assurance under a guarantee.
- Conservation and mission framing surface as supporting beats in paragraph 2 or later, never as the hook or as the guarantee frame. Opening a T4 underwrite with mission-speak reads as performative to a committee evaluating a Year-1 breakeven.
