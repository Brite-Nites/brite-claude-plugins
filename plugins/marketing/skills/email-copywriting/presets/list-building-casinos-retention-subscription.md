## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't pitch gaming-floor, pit, table games, slot arrays, count room, cage, or surveillance programmable-media. Brite's zone is the non-gaming footprint outside the NIGC and state-gaming-commission regulated perimeter. The retention-subscription motion integrates with the carded-player program, which lives in regulated data systems; Brite's programmable-media layer is the acquisition-funnel entry point, not the loyalty-program itself.
- Don't pitch Marketing or Facilities as primary. Marketing sells comp offers and loyalty-tier promotions; Facilities buys capex energy-retrofits. The retention-subscription decision surface is sub-type-dependent: GM + VP Casino Operations (regional commercial), Casino GM + Executive Officer Gaming and Hospitality + Tribal Council (tribal Class III), Director of Casino Operations (racinos), Regional VP of Operations (riverboat or multi-property). Director of F and B is shared secondary.
- Don't anchor on Las Vegas Strip or Atlantic City Boardwalk flagships as standalone framing. Strip integrated-resort loyalty programs (Caesars Rewards, M life, Wynn Rewards) run at a scale and capital norm above in-scope tier; name them only paired with an in-scope anchor (Seminole Hard Rock Hollywood, Foxwoods, Yaamava, Choctaw, Monarch Black Hawk) in the same sentence.
- No "luxury", "opulent", "high-roller-only", or "VIP-only" buzzwords. Consumer-casino warm-glow vocabulary zeroes out credibility on a Casino GM evaluating loyalty-tier conversion math.
- Use loyalty-program vocabulary, not consumer-casino vocabulary. Operator terms (carded-player attach, Unity tier or Caesars Rewards or M life tier differential, loyalty-tier conversion rate, compression-week premium, carded-player segment cohort) signal Brite has done the homework. Consumer-casino terms (high roller, whale, VIP suite, comp-hound) erode credibility.
- No "experience upgrade" standalone buzzword. The Offer B motion is infrastructure-as-acquisition-funnel, not aesthetic refresh. Use the specific metric (loyalty-tier conversion delta, carded-player attach lift, year-two renewal upside) the buyer tracks.
- No generic "ROI uplift" without a named loyalty-tier-specific metric. Offer B economics are specific: loyalty-tier conversion rate delta, carded-player attach rate lift, Unity-tier or peer-tier differential adoption, year-round architectural amortization ratio.
- Lead with loyalty-tier conversion or carded-player attach economic outcome; tribal-sovereignty, destination-branding, and press-worthy legacy live in paragraph 2 or later, not the hook.
- Frame Moment Factory as ceiling reference only. Resorts World Las Vegas GLOW and Tropicana Atlantic City are flagship-tier engagements above in-scope-tier norms; the Offer B motion is structurally different (ambient year-round infrastructure tied to loyalty-tier integration, not ticketed destination-pathway attraction).
- Frame MK Illumination as adjacent-not-competitive. MK's one US casino-adjacent install is a Strip retail-corridor; active-watch at attached-shopping-corridor build-outs at in-scope properties, not a direct competitor at the retention-subscription layer.
- Frame S4 Lights as Brite's partner, never a competitor.
- Don't pitch loyalty-program-integration as a technology sell. The Offer B motion is a commercial-structure sell (multi-year subscription, infrastructure ownership retained by Brite, property licenses year-round use) with loyalty-program-telemetry integration as enabling plumbing. The Casino GM buys the retention economics; the plumbing follows.
- Respect tribal sovereignty in framing at Tribal Class III prospects. Council-aware framing in body copy; address the tribe as a sovereign; expect a Tribal Council procurement gate in addition to property-level approval.
- Don't use urgency tactics. Multi-year subscription contracts run through capital committees, Tribal Councils, and Regional VP portfolios on multi-month cycles.
- Don't cite Brite Nites seasonal residential case studies. Cite Labs experiential or capital installations only.
- Don't reference installers or property management. Supply-excluded per handbook canon.
