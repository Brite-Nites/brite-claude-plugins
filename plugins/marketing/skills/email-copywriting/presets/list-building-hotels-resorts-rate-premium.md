## Hook (vertical-specific recency waterfall)

Anchor on a dual signal: existing program press (package-launch, seasonal brand announcement, Lodge-package-style direct-channel structure confirming the property already runs holiday programming) plus a Director of Revenue surface (new Director of Revenue appointment, STR quarterly comp-set commentary, revenue-tech stack announcement). ## Vertical anti-slop

- Don't position as a full-program replacement. The Rate-Premium Pilot specifically fits properties with a long-tenured incumbent vendor and a confident incumbent-relationship. Replacement framing ("let us take over from your current vendor") reads as vendor-aggressive and torpedoes the low-commitment pilot framing.
- Don't use vanity-metric guarantees (guest photo-shares, social-engagement counts, press mentions). The pilot is measured on revenue-manager scorecard metrics: peak-weekend ADR lift over comp-set baseline, direct-channel booking mix in the Q4 window, or F&B / spa attach rate on package guests.
- Don't name-drop Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, Ritz-Carlton, Four Seasons, or Waldorf Astoria as pilot peers. Use Hotel del Coronado (annual theme rotation as pilot-iteration precedent), Fairmont Scottsdale Princess (15-year YoY compounding as case-study anchor), or Callaway Resort & Gardens (package-economics precedent) as the proof surface.
- Don't address "VP Revenue Management" or "VP of Experience" in the salutation. Those titles do not exist at 100-400 key independents. Pitch Director of Revenue as primary (the numerate buyer) with GM as sign-authority, DOSM as influencer.
- Don't use "romantic", "ambiance", "magical", "enchanting", "dazzling", or "mesmerizing" anywhere in the hook or body. These are guest-brochure words and signal the vendor writes marketing copy, not revenue plans.
- Don't lead with wedding-venue or wedding-photo proof points. Events Sales is a separate buyer and a separate cost center; the pilot lands on the revenue-manager scorecard or it does not land at all.
- Don't use urgency language or "limited pilot slots" framing. The pilot structure is built on the property's 90-day Director-of-Revenue audit cycle, not on artificial Brite-side urgency. Urgency reads as a vendor-sales-manual tactic to a revenue manager who fields dozens of vendor pitches per quarter.
- Don't frame the commercial structure supplicantly ("we would love to earn your trust", "we are a new vendor eager to prove ourselves"). The voice is confident: "we underwrite the install. In exchange, we use the results, ADR data, and property name in our sales material." That framing establishes the trade cleanly.
