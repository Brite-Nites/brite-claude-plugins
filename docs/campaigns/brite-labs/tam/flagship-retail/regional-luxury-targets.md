# Regional / Independent Luxury & Destination Retail — Brite Labs Sourcing Targets

**Purpose:** Net-new prospecting targets for the flagship-retail TAM. These are **regional / independent** luxury and destination/experiential retailers across the Brite Labs footprint that no national prospecting pull surfaces. They are *adds* — none appear in `flagship_retail_people_2026-06-06` (that list captured national mall/luxury brands only).

**Footprint basis:** icp.json operating states (CA, AZ, CO, UT, FL, NC, NY, NJ, CT, VA, MD, PA) + ski-anchor states (WA, WY, ID, MT) + Dallas TX metro.

**Selection bar:** operates flagship-quality physical store(s); invests in store design / destination / in-store experience; regional or independent (national chains excluded). Web-verified currently-operating as of 2026-06.

**Fit basis:** Brite Labs sells custom permanent illuminated artwork / architectural lighting into design-led, experiential retail environments.
