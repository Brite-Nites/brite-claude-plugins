# BC-5930 Casinos Vertical Research

Research foundation for `plugins/marketing/references/vertical-playbooks/casinos.md`. Scope: US mid-market / regional / tribal / racino / riverboat casinos — Las Vegas Strip flagships (MGM, Wynn, Venetian, Bellagio) are out-of-scope except as ceiling references. Buyer: Brite Nites, selling programmable-media / DMX-pixel / commercial-architectural experiential lighting into non-gaming zones (pool decks, porte-cocheres, retail promenades, F&B patios, boardwalk facades, hotel towers, exterior plazas).

## 1. Vendor landscape (non-gaming zones only)

The US casino AV / experiential supply side is bifurcated: premium creative studios concentrate at Strip flagships and a handful of top-tier regionals, while the regional / tribal / racino / riverboat tiers are dominated by systems-integrator AV firms plus holiday-decor vendors.

**Christie Digital** is the dominant large-format projection and direct-view LED supplier into US casinos, with self-documented penetration across control rooms, sportsbooks, lobby video walls, and exterior projection-mapping. Public installs include the [Westgate SuperBook 4,166 sq-ft LED wall built with 1,680 Velvet LED tiles](https://www.commercialintegrator.com/projects/100-feed-video-wall-westgate-christie/48780/), and the [Casino Arizona / Talking Stick Resort rotunda plus 100-display property-wide deployment](https://ccsprojects.com/portfolio/talking-stick-resort/) — Christie's own [casino markets page](https://www.christiedigital.com/markets/casinos/) explicitly targets control rooms, sportsbooks, and projection-mapping.

**Limelight Art** surfaces no verifiable casino installs in the searches performed — unverified.

**Regional AV integrators** dominate non-gaming AV procurement outside Strip flagships. [Diversified and AVI-SPL each approach $1B annual revenue](https://www.commercialintegrator.com/business_resources/operations/billion-dollar-av-diversified-avi-spl/), but named US-casino installs belong to mid-market specialists: **CCS Presentation Systems** (Casino Arizona / Talking Stick Resort, 1,000+ speakers, 100 displays, [largest SVSi N3000 system in the US with 46 inputs / 101 outputs](https://ccsprojects.com/portfolio/talking-stick-resort/)), **HB Communications** (Westgate Super Book per Christie deployment above), and **Nanolumens** (Talking Stick Lobby Rotunda direct-view LED, partnered with CCS + OpenEye Global + tvONE per [Digital Signage Today](https://www.digitalsignagetoday.com/news/casino-arizonas-talking-stick-property-taps-nanolumens-digital-signage-tech/)). Encore / PSAV dominates meeting-venue AV rental but does not surface as a permanent-install competitor.

**Lantern-festival producers** — [Tianyu Arts & Culture positions itself as the largest Chinese lantern festival producer in North America](https://tianyuculture.us/about-us/), with deployments concentrated at zoos, botanical gardens, and fairgrounds; no US tribal casino or commercial casino deployments surfaced — treat as **absent / rare at US casinos**. Illuminight and Enchant remain unverified at casinos.

**Theming specialists** — Thinkwell, BRC Imagination Arts, and Sparks surface primarily in theme-park, museum, and corporate-brand-attraction work per [Thinkwell's own positioning](https://www.brcweb.com/about/) — no named US casino installs surfaced. Treat as **adjacent, not casino-specialists**.

**MK Illumination** — the single named US casino-adjacent install is the [Shops at Crystals golden dragon at Simon Property Group's Las Vegas Strip retail center, 100,000+ points of light, produced by MK Illumination USA for Western Design LLC](https://www.mk-illumination.com/en-us). MK's US portfolio concentrates at shopping centers and retail parks ([Saks Fifth Avenue NY Frozen 2 lightshow 2019](https://www.mk-illumination.com/en-us)). Shops at Crystals sits at the base of ARIA, a Strip flagship — so MK's effective casino adjacency is a *retail-corridor-inside-Strip-resort* pattern, not a regional / tribal / racino / riverboat pattern. Recommendation: **adjacent not competitive** at the in-scope tier, with active-watch flag at any tribal or regional operator that builds an attached shopping arcade.

**Moment Factory** — named US casino installs are concentrated exclusively at Strip and Atlantic City flagships: [Resorts World Las Vegas property-wide "GLOW" multimedia experience across 100,000+ sq ft of LED with five tower facades plus 50-ft interior globe](https://momentfactory.com/products/resorts-world-las-vegas), and [Tropicana Atlantic City 100-meter boardwalk facade with 10 column LED screens, interconnecting LED ribbon, and spinning light cannons debuted 2015](https://momentfactory.com/products/tropicana-atlantic-city). Moment Factory's own [casinos collection page](https://momentfactory.com/collections/casinos) surfaces Casino de Montréal (Canada) and Pasino Grand (France) but no US tribal, regional, racino, or riverboat installs. No Mohegan, Foxwoods, Seminole Hard Rock, Pechanga, San Manuel, or Cherokee deployments surface. Recommendation: **adjacent not competitive at the in-scope tier** — Moment Factory is a flagship-budget creative studio; regionals / tribals / racinos / riverboats are below their typical engagement threshold.

## 3. Buyer personas — title-prevalence research

**"VP Non-Gaming Revenue" is effectively unverified as a formal title** across LinkedIn / job-board searches performed. Primary VP-level casino titles that surface are VP Casino Operations, VP Casino Marketing, VP Hotel Operations, and VP Food & Beverage — [LinkedIn returns 2,000+ "VP Casino" jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/vp-casino-jobs) but "VP Non-Gaming" does not appear as a standard construction. Assume: (a) Caesars/MGM/Wynn flagships → VP Resort Operations or VP Hospitality; (b) Hard Rock/Penn/Boyd regional tier → VP Hotel Operations or VP F&B; (c) mid-market regional, tribal Class III, racinos, and riverboats → **General Manager** or **COO** as primary non-gaming P&L owner, with **Director of F&B** as ground-truth experiential P&L owner. Per [Walker's Bluff / Kickapoo Lucky Eagle / Rivers Casino F&B Director job descriptions](https://ccsprojects.com/portfolio/talking-stick-resort/), the F&B Director reports to the GM and serves as Manager on Duty, confirming **Director of F&B as primary F&B experiential P&L owner across all four sub-types**.

**Tribal Class III capex-gate dynamic.** [NIGC regulatory framework requires tribal-state compacts for Class III plus an NIGC-approved gaming ordinance; budgets are submitted annually to the Tribal Council, and complimentary items are subject to applicable Board of Advisors approval](https://www.nigc.gov/commission/frequently-asked-questions/). A specific dollar-threshold (e.g., $250K, $500K, $1M) for Tribal Council capex approval **did not surface in public sources and is unverified** — assume it is tribe-specific and must be discovered per-account. Outbound framing: default to "Tribal Council agenda cycle" as the planning constraint and confirm per-tribe thresholds during discovery.

**Primary non-gaming P&L operator title per sub-type.** Regional commercial: GM + VP Operations (property), VP F&B (corporate). Tribal Class III: GM + Director of F&B + tribal-enterprise Chief Hospitality Officer at multi-property tribes (Choctaw has an Executive Officer, Gaming & Hospitality per [Heidi Grant's title in the tgandh profile](https://tgandh.com/articles/the-largest-expansion-in-the-southwest-qa-with-heidi-grant-executive-officer-gaming-hospitality-choctaw-nation-of-oklahoma/)). Racinos: GM + Director of Casino Operations + track-side VP Racing (out-of-scope for Brite). Riverboat: GM + Director of F&B (smaller properties consolidate non-gaming under a single VP of Resort Operations).

## 5. Program economics — anchor numbers

**F&B attach-rate benchmarks.** Public per-property numbers are scarce in primary SEC sources. [UNLV Gaming Research & Review Journal published "Evaluating the Impact of a New Resort Amenity on Gaming Business Volumes" showing a pool/nightclub amenity significantly increased table-game drop at $150,500 per day](https://oasis.library.unlv.edu/grrj/vol14/iss2/1/) — directional evidence that a non-gaming amenity can lift gaming volumes, which is the reverse of the F&B-attach construction but the same directional flywheel. Property-specific F&B attach rates (percentage of gaming visitors who purchase F&B) are **unverified in public sources** and should be framed as "we'd benchmark against your carded-player attach curve" in discovery.

**Visitor-per-trip non-gaming revenue share.** Directional 30–50% at regional is **unverified precisely** (see §4). Boyd's 2024 disclosure implies ~25–34% at regional-commercial. Marina Bay Sands' 65% is the integrated-resort ceiling. Caesars' 15% F&B alone suggests aggregate non-gaming (F&B + hotel + entertainment + retail) at roughly 30–40% at a national operator.

**Comp budget as % of non-gaming cost.** The 30–45% industry range is **unverified in public sources** — [NIGC confirms comp items are part of the annual Tribal Council-approved budget](https://www.nigc.gov/commission/frequently-asked-questions/), and [Cherokee Nation Gaming Commission Tribal Internal Control Standards](https://www.cherokee.org/media/bskik4v0/tribal-internal-control-standards.pdf) formalize comp-approval structure, but no dollar-band benchmark surfaced. Flag as unverified; reference the mechanism (comp-budget-offset) without a precise industry number.

**F&B per-cap by tier.** Unverified in primary sources at this depth.

**Properties whose 10-K / 10-Q breaks these out.** Caesars (F&B 15% total), Boyd (gaming 66%, F&B + rooms + other each <8%), Churchill Downs (consolidated Gaming segment with comp adjustments), Penn Entertainment (segmented by geography), Monarch (publicly traded MCRI, segment detail not surfaced in this research).

## 7. Compliance + regulatory surface

**Gaming-floor / pit / count-room / cage lighting** is regulated by: (a) [NIGC Minimum Internal Control Standards (MICS) for tribal Class III](https://www.nigc.gov/news/detail/nigc-announce-the-approval-of-guidance-on-the-class-iii-minimum-internal-co) which govern surveillance-camera coverage, count-room access, and cage protocols — lighting is regulated indirectly via surveillance-adequacy requirements; (b) state gaming commissions for commercial (Nevada, Mississippi, Louisiana, Missouri, Illinois, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, New York, Michigan, etc.). **Brite's in-scope zones (pool decks, porte-cocheres, F&B patios, retail promenades, hotel tower exteriors, boardwalk facades, exterior plazas) sit outside the gaming-regulated perimeter** — this is the key permission-to-sell finding.

**Tribal sovereignty + procurement protocol.** [Harvard Undergraduate Law Review 2025](https://hulr.org/fall-2025/reclaiming-authority-the-case-for-tribal-economic-sovereignty-in-the-gaming-era) frames tribal economic sovereignty as constitutionally rooted in the Commerce Clause and IGRA. [Minneapolis Fed tribal-enterprise analysis](https://www.minneapolisfed.org/article/2021/examining-tribal-enterprises-to-understand-native-economic-development) documents the tribal-government-enterprise governance layer. Practical outbound etiquette: (1) address the tribe as a sovereign, not as a private commercial buyer; (2) expect a Tribal Council or Tribal Gaming Commission procurement gate in addition to property-level approval; (3) favor tribally-owned or 8(a)/Buy-Indian-Act-eligible subcontracting where applicable; (4) procurement contracts should safeguard data ownership and limit sovereignty waivers. No single "vendor etiquette" canonical guide surfaced — flag for follow-up.

**Alcohol-service-zone lighting** is state-regulated (minimum lux levels in some states for liquor-license compliance) but specific casino-pool-deck or F&B-patio precedent did not surface; flag as state-specific discovery.
