## task-1 — Orchestrator markdown that implements a Q-lock IS the canonical body-text spec; ASCII diagrams are auxiliary

**Decision:** When an orchestrator command (`/flow:start-project`, `/flow:retrofit-project`, `/flow:add-*`, ...) implements a locked design from the interview record (Q-lock at memory:NNN), the body-text gate/phase definitions are the canonical spec. ASCII diagrams are auxiliary visualizations and may be layout-misleading without being incorrect. Independent reviewers — and especially **review-agent validators** — must consult the upstream Q-lock before promoting a "contradiction" between diagram and body to P1; the body's labels follow the lock, not the diagram. The Opus validator in this session caught a code-reviewer P1 finding (confidence 9/10) that flagged "G3 and G4 both labeled `(3→4)`" as a bug — the Q37 sub-decision 3 lock (memory:688) explicitly locks both as `(3→4)` by design ("G4 fires alongside G3 OR after G3 if user pauses"). The validator's lock-read downgraded P1 → P3 (the diagram's positioning of G4 between Phase 4 and Phase 5 boxes IS misleading; a clarifying blockquote was added). Lesson: for orchestrator markdown implementing a Q-lock, the reviewer that does NOT consult the lock will generate false-positive contradictions at high confidence.
