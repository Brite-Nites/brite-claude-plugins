## task-1 — REFINEMENT pattern-choice: convergent-dogfood is a milestone signal, NOT a strict-terminator signal

**Decision:** When a dogfood chain reaches convergent-termination (all keystone rows verified, zero blocking refutations, all enumerated follow-ups shipped — BC-7599's framing), the chain has reached a **milestone** of stability. It has NOT reached strict closure. A discoverable-tail of delayed-sibling issues may surface from prior rounds even after the convergent-dogfood declaration. The pattern signal is "this is a good place to step back" — not "no more work in this lineage is possible."

For BC-7980: BC-7599 (PR #285, 2026-05-11) declared the BC-5826 → BC-6785 launch-campaign dogfood chain TERMINATES at 24 shipped follow-ups via convergent-dogfood verdict. BC-7980 (PR #307, 2026-05-12 — next day) is a **delayed-discoverable Bundle-2 sibling** to BC-7598. It surfaced because BC-6785 round-5's R-28 (sibling-endpoint case-asymmetry probe) was inconclusive at round-5 close (`call_api` is JSON-only and couldn't exercise the multipart `POST /api/leads/bulk/csv` endpoint). The spec-correctness gaps F-R-28a + F-R-28b shipped in BC-7597; the case-asymmetry rule itself remained unverified and was carry-over investigation. A subsequent curl multipart spike (2026-05-12) confirmed storage-time case preservation, which generated this issue's actionable scope.

**Why this matters for chain-state-tracking:** BC-7599 task-3 was filed as promotion-eligible at 8/10 confidence for "convergent-dogfood architecture-9 — 24 follow-up issues / 24 shipped / 5 rounds." The promotion claim was *almost* right but conflated two adjacent concepts: (a) the chain reached a *stable milestone* where all enumerated work shipped (correct), and (b) the chain *terminates* — no further sibling issues can surface (incorrect; BC-7980 disproves within 24 hours).

**Refined framing for the promotion candidate:**
- **Convergent-dogfood milestone** = "all keystone rows verified + zero blocking refutations + all enumerated follow-ups shipped." This is a true predicate and a meaningful checkpoint signal.
- **Chain-terminator** = "no further sibling issues will surface from this lineage." This is NOT a derivable predicate from convergent-dogfood. Delayed-discoverable siblings can surface when (a) earlier-round inconclusive probes get unblocked by independent work (here: curl multipart capability outside `call_api`), (b) the original issue body's "open questions" remain answerable after the convergent-dogfood walk closes, (c) sibling-endpoint patterns from established rules invite probes the original walk didn't enumerate.

**Category:** pattern-choice (refinement of BC-7599 task-3 convergent-dogfood candidate)

**Confidence:** 7/10 — REFINEMENT of an existing pattern; load-bearing-pending until a 2nd convergent-dogfood chain demonstrates either (a) genuine strict-closure with no delayed siblings within 30 days of declaration, or (b) another delayed-sibling surface that confirms milestone-not-closure framing.
