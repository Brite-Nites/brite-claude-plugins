## Company Overview & Flywheel

The Brite Company operates four interconnected brands in lighting and entertainment:

- **Brite Nites** — holiday/seasonal lighting installation for high-end residential. 30 years of field intelligence; the operational backbone.
- **Brite Supply** — products and marketplace for professional installers; every product born from a jobsite problem.
- **Brite Base** — FSM SaaS for holiday lighting companies, dogfooded internally.
- **Brite Labs** — custom creative productions and large-scale commercial lighting (the populated entity below).

The flywheel: Nites field intelligence → Supply products; Base software → more efficient ops → more intelligence; Nites residential reputation → Labs commercial doors; Labs projects → specialized Supply hardware needs.

## Entity: Brite Labs

### Positioning

Brite Labs is an **experiential events partner**: custom art pieces plus cost-effective sourcing and design for large-scale production events — Instagrammable wow factors. Two core motions:

1. **Seasonal experiential events** — signature holiday/seasonal programs with land-and-expand annual recurrence.
2. **Permanent facade upscaling / custom illuminated artwork** — year-round custom installations, now a core motion (not seasonal-only).

Brand line: **"Made for the moment."** / **"CREATE THE MOMENT"** — signature lighting moments across hospitality, higher-ed, sports, and beyond (britelabs.io).

### ICP

- **Who:** commercial venues, municipalities, entertainment properties needing experiential or large-scale lighting.
- **Qualifying floor:** project budget **$50K+**; the real prize is land-and-expand annual program recurrence.
- **Buying pattern:** committee-driven; buyer titles vary heavily. Recurring personas: **Owner, GM, VP Marketing**, plus ~10 adjacent titles (Brand leadership, Director of Customer Experience, etc.). Use vertical canonicals (`plugins/marketing/data/canonicals/`) for per-vertical persona slugs.
- **Active verticals (all focus-tier):** hotels-resorts, retail/shopping-centers, ski resorts, casinos, universities, healthcare, municipal/tribal, car dealerships.
- **Budget timing:** most targets plan and commit budget in **Q1 for the entire year**. Post-Q1 outreach is next-fiscal-year planning (seasonal events or permanent facade upscaling). Occasional unused-budget carry-over creates off-cycle openings.

### Voice Delta (overrides company-wide pricing rule)

**Avoid ALL ROI claims.** Instead, make sound conjectures supported by market data points, attached to personalized offers around events that become revenue-increasing opportunities.

Experience-economy proof stack:

- **$144B** global immersive market (2025), projected **$412B+** by 2030
- **78%** of millennials prefer spending on experiences over material goods
- **24.9%** CAGR, U.S. immersive entertainment market

Voice exemplar: *"The experience economy isn't coming. It's here. Guests don't visit spaces. They seek moments worth sharing. Properties that deliver those moments see measurable returns."*

Tone: creative-studio energy ("CREATE THE MOMENT") toward GM/VP Marketing committees — creative partner, never commodity vendor.

### Competitive Frame

Labs has **no single-category competitor**. Adjacent categories (event production agencies, AV/staging, sign fabricators, local lighting contractors) only win when a buyer cuts corners or accepts **managing a dozen vendors** for a full production. Differentiator: single full-production partner — custom fabrication + cost-effective sourcing + design + install + annual program management under one roof, backed by 30 years of Brite field operations.
